# Donna Haraway

**Born 1944.**

## The claim in one line

The view from nowhere and the view from everywhere are **the same evasion**; knowledge is always situated, and naming its position makes it **accountable** rather than weaker.

## Quote

> I am arguing for politics and epistemologies of location, positioning, and situating.

## Where she fits in the course

Haraway offers a third option when you get stuck between neutral science and total relativism. The course spends six weeks removing the neutral view and rules out the relativist shrug, so both of those are dead ends. [[concepts/situated-knowledge|Situated knowledge]] is the way out that does not require taking either back.

She also extends naturally from [[entities/tobias-rees|Rees]] in [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w09-is-the-human-and-machine-line-essential-or-contingent|W09]] if you want to keep going.
