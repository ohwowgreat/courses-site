# Benjamin Bratton

**Born 1968.**

## The claim in one line

Computation is **planetary infrastructure** rather than a set of tools, and AI may be the only way to know systems too large for any single mind to perceive.

## Quote to put on the board

> The Copernican trauma was not that we were displaced, but that we could only know it through an instrument.

## How the Oxbridge course uses him

He is the natural next step after [[entities/tobias-rees|Rees]] if you want to keep going.

Bratton reframes AI as **an instrument of perception rather than a rival to human thought**. This loops back to the thermometer in [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w03-can-measurement-reach-the-thing-itself|W03]]. If knowing through an instrument is the normal condition and not a compromise, the week-9 question changes shape: the interesting thing about a machine is not whether it thinks like us, but what it lets us see.

The board quote puts [[concepts/the-gap|the gap]] and the AI question in one line.

## Reading

*The Terraforming*, or the shorter Noema pieces. This is extension reading, not assigned.
