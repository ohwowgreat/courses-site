# Irmgard Emmelhainz

**Contemporary.**

## The claim in one line

The shared world has fragmented into **private, algorithmically shaped worlds**, which raises the question of whether a common ground can be found that does not depend on representation at all.

## The central question

> Can we share a world beyond representation?

This is her essay title and the session's central question.

## How the course uses her

She is the set text for week 12, and the most personally implicating reading in the course.

The question is philosophical, not a complaint about social media: not whether the feed is bad, but whether **sharing a world requires representation at all.**

If you answer "shared experience, not shared images — eating together, working together," that is the strongest available answer, and the question back is: **does that scale beyond a room?** If it does not, you have described a shared world or a small one, and saying which is the whole argument.
