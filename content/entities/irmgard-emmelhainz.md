# Irmgard Emmelhainz

**Contemporary.**

## The claim in one line

The shared world has fragmented into **private, algorithmically shaped worlds**. This raises the question of whether a common ground can be found that does not depend on representation at all.

## Quote to put on the board

> Can we share a world beyond representation?

Her essay title, and the session's central question.

## How the course uses her

Her essay is the set text for week 12, and the most personally implicating reading in the course.

Keep the question philosophical rather than letting it become a complaint about social media. The question is not whether the feed is bad. It is whether **sharing a world requires representation at all.**

If you answer "shared experience, not shared images: eating together, working together," you have given the strongest available answer. The question back is: **does that scale beyond a room?** If it does not, you have described a shared world or a small one, and saying which is the whole argument.
