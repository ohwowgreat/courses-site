# bell hooks

bell hooks is a representation theorist named in the course. She styled her name in lowercase, and that spelling is kept here.

## What Cambridge attributes to her

Cambridge lists her as one of the representation theorists you can study:

> "candidates need to learn the concept of Representation - best done with reference to theories of representation where there is a variety of student-friendly materials available - such as **Hall, Van Zoonen, hooks, Gilroy, Alvarado**." (2028 SoW, C2 Section A camera row)

Cambridge names her but does not state which concept to study her for.

## BNDS usage

In this course you study **the oppositional gaze**, hooks's idea of looking as refusal and rereading: "who the text never expected to be looking." You meet it in [[classes/media-studies/lesson-plans/9607-s1-lesson-12-the-gaze|L12]] on the Friday single lesson, where it closes the gaze arc and feeds CS10. The lesson sets a 300–400-word hooks extract in an accessible gloss, in English with Chinese support.
