# Hito Steyerl

**Born 1966.**

## The claim in one line

Image quality is not only a technical property. It is **a marker of value, access, and circulation**. Perspective shows the world from a point rather than showing the world.

## Key quote

> The poor image is a copy in motion.

## How the Oxbridge course uses her

Steyerl is **the hinge of the first unit.** She lets you use your own vocabulary of resolution and compression, then turns that vocabulary into a political argument. Her account of [[concepts/linear-perspective|linear perspective]] carries forward into [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w04-is-there-a-reality-behind-appearances|W04]], so she is the only thinker whose argument spans two units.

## Set text

"In Defense of the Poor Image", e-flux journal. Before [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w02-do-images-represent-reality-or-produce-it|W02]], write one paragraph on **the single sentence you found most arguable**. See [[classes/oxbridge/oxbridge-reading-list|the reading list]].
