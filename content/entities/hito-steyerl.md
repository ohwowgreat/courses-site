# Hito Steyerl

**Born 1966.**

## The claim in one line

Image quality is not only a technical property; it is **a marker of value, access, and circulation** — and perspective shows the world from a point rather than showing the world.

## Quote

> The poor image is a copy in motion.

## In the Oxbridge course

Steyerl opens the first unit. She lets you use your own vocabulary of resolution and compression, then turns that vocabulary into a political argument.

Her account of [[concepts/linear-perspective|linear perspective]] carries forward into [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w04-is-there-a-reality-behind-appearances|W04]], so her argument spans two units.

## Set text

"In Defense of the Poor Image", e-flux journal. Write one paragraph on **the single sentence you found most arguable** before [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w02-do-images-represent-reality-or-produce-it|W02]]. See [[classes/oxbridge/oxbridge-reading-list|the reading list]].
