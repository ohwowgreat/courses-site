# Manuel Alvarado

**Manuel Alvarado** is a theorist of representation named in the 2028 Scheme of Work.

## What Cambridge attributes to him

> "candidates need to learn the concept of Representation - best done with reference to theories of representation where there is a variety of student-friendly materials available - such as **Hall, Van Zoonen, hooks, Gilroy, Alvarado**." (2028 SoW, C2 Section A camera row)

The clause concerns representation of "class, age, gender, ethnicity." Alvarado is the standard A Level media-studies reference for racial-stereotype categories, usually taught alongside Hall and Gilroy.

## Status in the course

**Not currently taught at BNDS.** Like [[entities/paul-gilroy|Paul Gilroy]], he is a possible way to deepen the ethnicity strand of [[concepts/representation|Representation]] in the G12 year.
