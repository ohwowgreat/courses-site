# Liesbet van Zoonen

Liesbet van Zoonen is a representation theorist. Her work belongs to feminist media theory.

## What Cambridge attributes to her

Cambridge lists her among the theories of representation you can use to study the concept of Representation:

> "candidates need to learn the concept of Representation - best done with reference to theories of representation where there is a variety of student-friendly materials available - such as **Hall, Van Zoonen, hooks, Gilroy, Alvarado**." (2028 SoW, C2 Section A camera row)

Cambridge names her but does not say what to use her for. The clause covers representation of class, age, gender, and ethnicity. Van Zoonen is the standard media-studies reference for the gender strand.

## BNDS usage

In Lesson 09 you work on **gender as performance**, the idea that gender is produced through performance rather than being fixed. You apply it to two advertisements, before Mulvey's three looks are stated precisely. See the lesson plan: [[classes/media-studies/lesson-plans/9607-s1-lesson-12-the-gaze|L12]].
