# Neil Postman

Named, with [[entities/marshall-mcluhan|Marshall McLuhan]], as the origin of [[concepts/media-ecology|Media Ecology]].

## What Cambridge attributes to him

> "The notion of media ecology can be traced back to **Neil Postman** and **Marshall McLuhan**, but its contemporary relevance is clearly driven by the saturation of all aspects of contemporary existence with media and media technologies." (SoW p.25)

Cambridge names him as an origin of media ecology. It credits him with no specific idea, work, or argument.

## What is left standing

Cambridge gives media ecology as a **method** rather than a theory (SoW pp.25–26):

- historical contrast: "What did the media landscape look like before 1990?" set against your own media diary
- a premise: contemporary life is saturated with media and "mediatised"
- a temporal orientation: "contrasting the present with the past, and analysing the present in order to develop ideas about the future"

The before/after contrast does most of the analytical work. The exam rewards "informed, imaginative analysis" (SoW p.26) grounded in contemporary evidence. The syllabus also requires you to "use key theories of media studies" (Syllabus 2027 p.13).
