# Paul Gilroy

Paul Gilroy is a representation theorist named in the 2028 Scheme of Work.

## What Cambridge attributes to him

> "candidates need to learn the concept of Representation - best done with reference to theories of representation where there is a variety of student-friendly materials available - such as **Hall, Van Zoonen, hooks, Gilroy, Alvarado**." (2028 SoW, C2 Section A camera row)

This is all Cambridge says about him. The clause is about the representation of "class, age, gender, ethnicity." Gilroy is the standard A Level media-studies reference for the ethnicity strand, alongside Hall, hooks and Alvarado.

## Status in the course

Gilroy is not currently taught at BNDS. The S1 representation block ([[classes/media-studies/unit-plans/9607-s1-unit-4-representation|U4]]) uses Hall, van Zoonen, Mulvey and hooks. He may be taught in the G12 year, where the class, age, gender and ethnicity axes recur across both exam components.
