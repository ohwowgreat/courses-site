# Oxbridge W08: Can the Body Be Trusted?

[[classes/oxbridge/oxbridge|Oxbridge]] · [[classes/oxbridge/unit-plans/oxbridge-unit-4-the-body-and-the-machine|Unit 4: The Body and the Machine]] · Week 8 of 14 · [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w07-argument-workshop|← W07]] | [[classes/oxbridge/lesson-plans/oxbridge-w09-is-the-human-and-machine-line-essential-or-contingent|W09 →]]

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Core question | You know you are cold because you feel it. Is feeling more reliable than any system, or is the body just another framework? |
| Duration | 45 minutes, [[classes/oxbridge/oxbridge-session-structure-template\|standard template]] |
| Reading | **None.** One quotation sent in advance |
| Sent ahead | "The body is our general medium for having a world" — [[entities/maurice-merleau-ponty\|Merleau-Ponty]] |
| Carried out | What can you do that a machine cannot? |

## Opening prompt (5 minutes)

> Describe being cold without using the word cold, and without using a number.

Two minutes, silent.

## Movement 1 · elicit (10 minutes)

- Can you be wrong about being cold?
- If a thermometer says 22 degrees and you are shivering, **which one is wrong?**

## Movement 2 · complicate (15 minutes)

Four cases, introduced one at a time. Each closes a different escape route.

| Case | The question |
|---|---|
| **Phantom limb** | A person feels pain in a leg that is not there. Is the pain real? Is it accurate? |
| **Chronic pain without injury** | The signal fires with nothing to report. Is the body lying, or reporting honestly about itself? |
| **Synaesthesia** | Someone hears color. Are they perceiving more of the world, or differently? |
| **Adaptation** | Move from a cold room to a warm one and the same water feels hot, then neutral. **Which reading was correct?** |

Questions:

- Is the body a window onto the world, or a medium that shapes what can appear in it?
- Merleau-Ponty says the body is the medium for having a world. If the medium is wrong, **are you wrong about the world, or do you have a different world?**
- Is a blind person perceiving less of the world, or a different world?

## Movement 3 · commit (10 minutes)

The Spinoza reframe:

> We do not yet know what a body can do.

Stop asking what a body **is** and ask what a body **can do**. Does that dissolve the problem or dodge it?

Each student states a position: the body is an instrument to be corrected, **or** the body is the condition of there being anything to correct.

## Closing question (5 minutes)

> What can you do that a machine cannot?
