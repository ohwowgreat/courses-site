# A Level Art & Design · S1 Course Skills

[[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|S1 Assessment Register]] · the ten Course Skills checks of the portfolio semester

The four attainments and the Final have their own pages. The ten **Course Skills** (CS) checks are the smaller instruments between them, and this page holds them all: what each looks at, when, and what it practices. In a studio course the checks are not separate tasks. Each one collects or inspects work you were producing anyway, so this page is really a map of the semester's checkpoints.

## How these count

**Course Skills is 20% of your grade**, from the ten checks below, with **72 hours' notice**. Every one is marked on **evidence of observation and honest annotation, not polish**, and every one practices a named attainment: CS1 to CS4 build toward A1, CS5 to CS7 toward A2, CS8 and CS9 toward A3, CS10 toward A4. What is in your sketchbook or on your pages when the check falls is what is checked. You do not make anything specially for a CS.

## Toward A1: the recording checks

### CS1 · Formal elements set and Recording Page 1

**Mon 2026-09-14, sketchbook.** The week's study set of **formal elements**, line, tone and form, texture, color, plus **Recording Page 1** composed from it. The page must argue something, not display exercises: selection and sequence over coverage. Returned inside five working days.

### CS2 · Intention and mind map

**Fri 2026-09-18, written annotation.** Your **intention statement** in the three-part frame, the A3 **mind map** at least three branches past the first idea, and the **thumbnail sheet** with one composition circled and every rejection carrying its one-line reason. The rejection habit starts here, and the examiner reads it all year.

### CS3 · Sustained-study progress check

**Thu 2026-09-24, walked, not collected.** A look at the **sustained study** mid-carry: is it one study carried without restarts, with proportion checked, tone mapped, edges found by value. It falls on the session when A1 is announced.

### CS4 · Recording pages build check

**Wed 2026-09-30, sketchbook.** The **recording pages** built and sequenced so a stranger reads the recording in order: intention, studies, reference. Checked before the break so A1's assembly is a check, not a scramble.

## Toward A2: the exploration checks

### CS5 · The twelve experiments

**Fri 2026-10-16, sketchbook.** All twelve **media experiments** documented, each labeled with the question it tested and what it showed. At least one experiment must have failed, and its annotation says so and says why. A safe dozen is the one way to lose marks here.

### CS6 · Stage 3 page and the selection note

**Fri 2026-10-23, written annotation.** The **Stage 3 page** reading as reasoning, experiments, decision, trial, refinement in order, plus the **media selection note** that names the chosen media in AO language: what this medium does to this theme. If a field trip takes the Friday, it collects on Monday.

### CS7 · Artist pages

**Fri 2026-10-30, written annotation.** The **Artist 1 pages**: analysis, meaning and context, method study, personal response, influence note. Marked on whether the influence shows in your own work and whether the writing survives the strike test: no sentence that could describe any artist.

## Toward A3: the development checks

### CS8 · Both compositions

**Fri 2026-11-20, photographed and laid out.** **Compositions 1 and 2** side by side, documented honestly. Composition 2 must be genuinely the rejected alternative at equal effort, because the comparison is the evidence and an unequal pair proves nothing.

### CS9 · Targeted tests and the critique response

**Fri 2026-11-27, written annotation.** The **targeted tests** run only on what the critique left unresolved, each labeled with the question it answered, plus the acted-on **critique response**. No test exists because it was enjoyable.

## Toward A4: the production check

### CS10 · Mid-production review reflection

**Fri 2026-12-18, written.** The **mid-production review** of the final outcome against its plan, mid-build, while change is still possible: what changes, in what order, and why. The ranked list is the point, because three days of resolve run remain and they go to the top of it.

## Semesters 2 to 4

The later semesters run their own CS sequences toward the **Personal Investigation** and the **Externally Set Assignment**. Their registers carry the dates.
