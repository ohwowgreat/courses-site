## Objectives

This assessment asks you to show that you can:

1. Photograph your outcome as the version an examiner will actually see. The photograph **is** the work as far as submission is concerned.
2. Sequence the whole journey so it reads in order without you narrating it: theme, recording, exploration, artists, development, outcome.
3. Cut what does not earn its place, and say what you cut.
4. Check your own portfolio against all four AO descriptors, in writing, before it goes.
5. Submit a portfolio that realizes the intention you set in Week 1, or that shows honestly where the intention changed and why.

## The task

A4 is the semester's submission. It is the only attainment marked on all four AOs, because it is the whole portfolio rather than a slice of it.

What you submit:

- **The resolved final outcome**, photographed properly: rig, light, full shot, detail passes, cropped, corrected, named and filed to the template.
- **The complete supporting portfolio**: intention, recording pages, sustained study, media experiments, media selection, scale trial, both artist studies, the bridge piece, both compositions, targeted tests, evaluations, and the final composition plan.
- **In sequence**, laid out as a run order that a stranger can follow.

You submit all work digitally. Cambridge takes no hyperlinks and no QR codes. Annotation is assessed within AO3 and must be **typed**.

## Instructions

**Submission week runs Mon 01-04 to Fri 01-08, and every day has a job.**

1. **Mon 01-04.** Photograph the outcome, then prepare the images: crop, correct, name, file to the template.
2. **Tue 01-05.** Sequence the portfolio. Lay the full run order out and walk it once. Then a peer walks it and tells you where a stranger stumbles.
3. **Wed 01-06.** Edit and clean. Cut what does not earn its place and **log every cut.** Then do the final AO check: all four descriptors, in writing, signed.
4. **Thu 01-07.** Submit, in session. **Nothing is edited after.**
5. **Fri 01-08.** Submission is acknowledged, you write your semester reflection, and the winter homework is set.

**The rule that matters most: no edits after submission.** Work not in the portfolio on Thursday is not in the portfolio.

**If you are absent.** Contact your teacher **at least 24 hours before** the deadline. Request extensions 24 hours ahead. Without advance contact the mark may be 0, even for genuine illness (AL Student Guide p.8). The school's usual late-work cutoff, roughly three weeks before the last school day of the semester, falls inside the winter break for S1, so do not rely on it.

## Rubric

**All four AOs, 60 marks**, from Cambridge's Component 1 grid. Full descriptors for every level: [[classes/a-level-art-design/9479-assessment-criteria|9479 Assessment Criteria]].

| AO | What it judges here | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **AO1 Record** | Selection of subject matter from a variety of sources, methods of recording, intention communicated through critical reflection | 15 |
| **AO2 Explore** | Selection of media and processes, understanding of skills and visual elements, experimentation that reviews and refines | 15 |
| **AO3 Develop** | Purposeful connections, a focused investigation with analytical and critical understanding, exploration of visual elements, reflection on strengths and weaknesses | **20** |
| **AO4 Present** | Realizing intentions, and applying visual language to communicate ideas | 10 |

### AO4 Present, the one A4 adds

The other three AOs have been sampled at A1, A2 and A3. AO4 is assessed for the first time here, because it is a property of the portfolio as a whole.

| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | 9–10 | **Accomplished** portfolio that **fully** realises intentions · perceptive application of visual language to communicate ideas |
| **4** | 7–8 | Confident portfolio that effectively realises intentions · confident application of visual language |
| **3** | 5–6 | Competent portfolio that realises intentions · relevant application of visual language |
| **2** | 3–4 | Adequate portfolio that **partly** realises intentions · some application of visual language |
| **1** | 1–2 | Basic portfolio that does **not fully** realise intentions · limited application of visual language |
| **0** | 0 | No creditable response |

AO4 is where sequencing and editing are paid for. Cambridge asks that the work be "thoughtfully and appropriately selected and presented" so that ideas and progress read clearly. A portfolio holding strong work in a confusing order loses marks here that the work itself had earned.

AO4 is 10 marks of 60 in this specification, **down** from the old equal split. Do not spend more time on presentation than on development, which is worth double.

Marks are curved under [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the BNDS framework]] rather than reported raw. Cambridge itself will mark this portfolio as a whole out of 60 at the end of the course. A4's mark is the school's forecast of that.

## After the sit

- **Marking window Thu 2027-01-07 to Wed 2027-01-20**, nine working days. Grades and comments are due Wed 2027-01-20.
- W20 (from Mon 01-11) is the school exam window. There are **no Art and Design classes** that week.
- Portfolios come back in **W21**, one to one, Tuesday to Thursday.
- Winter vacation runs 2027-01-24 to 2027-02-21. Your winter homework is three candidate Personal Investigation themes with annotated references, due Mon 2027-02-22: the [[classes/a-level-art-design/9479-c3-winter-brief|C3 Winter Brief]].
