# A Level Art & Design · S1 CS3: Sustained-Study Progress Check

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Thu 2026-09-24, walked and recorded on the rota sheet |
| What | A look at the sustained study mid-carry |
| Note | Not collected. It is checked where it sits |
| Feeds | A1 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Carry one study through without restarting it
2. Check proportion before committing to detail
3. Find an edge by its value rather than by drawing a line around it

## Description

This is a walked check, not a hand-in. Mid-carry, I come to your study and we look at it together against three things:

- **One study, carried.** Not three fresh starts. A restart resets the learning.
- **Proportion checked** early, and checked again.
- **Tone mapped**, with edges found by value rather than outlined.

It is recorded on the rota sheet as we go. A1 is announced in the same session, so what the check finds is what you have three weeks to act on.

## Guidelines

- Bring the study you have actually been carrying, not a tidier one begun last night.
- Do not restart because it is going badly. Fix it in place; that is the skill.
- Block the large tonal masses before any detail.
- Where two shapes meet, ask what value each is, not where the line goes.
- Be ready to say what you would change. The check is a conversation.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | One study visibly carried over the whole period. Proportion holds. Tone is mapped across the study, and edges are made by value shifts. |
| **Secure** | Carried without restarts, proportion broadly right, tonal mapping under way but uneven. |
| **Developing** | Evidence of restarting, or proportion drifting uncorrected, or the study is outlined and filled rather than built from value. |
| **Not yet** | No sustained study in progress, or it has been begun repeatedly and abandoned. |

Nothing is collected. The rota sheet is the record.
