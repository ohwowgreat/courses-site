# A Level Art & Design · S1 CS2: Intention and Mind Map

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Due | Fri 2026-09-18, written annotation |
| What | Intention statement, A3 mind map, thumbnail sheet |
| Feeds | A1, and every rejection note you write this year |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. State an intention in the three-part frame
2. Push a mind map past your first idea
3. Give a reason for every option you reject

## Description

Three pieces of writing and drawing, due together.

Your **intention statement** in the three-part frame: what you are making, what it is about, and how you plan to make it.

The **A3 mind map**, taken **at least three branches past the first idea**. The first idea is almost never the one worth making. The map is where you prove you looked further.

The **thumbnail sheet**, with one composition circled, and **every rejection carrying its one-line reason**.

That last habit starts here and the examiner reads it all year. A rejected thumbnail with a reason is evidence. A rejected thumbnail without one is a doodle.

## Guidelines

- Intention statement: three or four sentences, no more.
- Mind map on A3. Smaller paper produces smaller thinking.
- Count your branches. If you stopped at two, you stopped too early.
- Six thumbnails minimum on the sheet.
- Circle one. Not two, not none.
- Every uncircled thumbnail gets one line: why not this one.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Intention is specific and testable. The map visibly travels past the obvious. Every rejection reason names something about the composition, not about effort or taste. |
| **Secure** | All three parts present, the map goes past the first idea, and most rejections carry a real reason. |
| **Developing** | Intention is vague ("something about nature"), the map stays close to the first thought, or rejection reasons are missing on several thumbnails. |
| **Not yet** | A piece is missing, or no composition is chosen, or nothing is rejected on the record. |

The rejection habit is the one being built. It is worth more later than the thumbnails are.
