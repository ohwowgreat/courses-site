# A Level Art & Design · S1 CS10: Mid-Production Review Reflection

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Due | Fri 2026-12-18, written |
| What | The final outcome reviewed against its plan, mid-build |
| Deliverable | A ranked list of changes |
| Feeds | A4 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Review your own work against its plan while change is still possible
2. Rank changes rather than listing them
3. Spend limited remaining time on what matters most

## Description

The review of the final outcome **against its plan, mid-build, while change is still possible**. Not at the end, when the only honest reflection is regret.

Three questions: **what changes, in what order, and why.**

**The ranked list is the point.** Three days of resolve run remain and they go to the top of that list. An unranked list of nine improvements is the same as no list, because you cannot do nine things in three days.

## Guidelines

- Put the plan next to the outcome and look at both.
- List what differs. Everything, at first.
- Then rank. Number them. Ties are not allowed.
- For the top three, say why they are top: what does fixing this buy?
- Be realistic about three days. If item one takes all three, say so.
- The bottom of the list is a decision too: these will not be fixed, deliberately.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Honest comparison against the plan, a genuinely ranked list with reasons, and a realistic account of what the remaining time can cover. What is being let go is named. |
| **Secure** | Comparison made and list ranked, reasons present but thin, time estimate optimistic. |
| **Developing** | A list of improvements with no ranking, or a review that defends the outcome rather than examining it. |
| **Not yet** | No comparison against the plan, or written after the work was finished. |

Written while change is possible. That is what separates this from an evaluation.
