## Objectives

This assessment asks you to show that you can:

1. Experiment widely enough that the media choice you eventually make is a decision rather than a default.
2. Say **why** you selected the media you did, in terms of what you are trying to communicate.
3. Set two artists against each other and name the **tension** between them, not their similarities.
4. Make a bridge piece that visibly carries something from the artist study into your own work.
5. Reflect critically on which experiments failed, and what the failure told you.
6. Leave the review with **one direction you can defend in two minutes.**

## The task

A2 reviews everything Unit 3 produced. You make nothing new for it.

What is on the table:

- **Twelve media experiments**, documented in the sketchbook.
- **Your media selection**, with the written note saying what you are taking forward and why.
- **The scale trial.**
- **Artist pages for both artists**, with your analysis.
- **The bridge piece**, made in one sustained session and finished across the Juniors Days gap.

The bridge piece is the load-bearing item. It is the object that proves the artist study changed your work rather than sitting beside it.

A2 is marked on **two** AO columns, which is why it carries the most marks of any milestone this semester: AO2 Explore (15) and AO3 Develop (20).

## Instructions

**Before the review.** You plan and sign off the bridge piece on Mon 11-02, make it on Tue 11-03, and finish it during the six-day Juniors Days gap on the schedule you dated in your sketchbook. The gap is the finishing window, not a break from it.

**On the day.**

1. Lay out all five items in the order you want them read.
2. The review happens in session, with you present.
3. Nothing is added during the review.

**After.** A2 comes back banded on Tue 11-10. You write **one sentence** naming the direction you are taking into Unit 4, and defend it in a two-minute stand-up to the room. A direction you cannot defend out loud is not yet a direction.

**If you are absent.** Contact your teacher **at least 24 hours before**. Illness needs a doctor's note. Without advance contact the mark may be 0, even for genuine illness (AL Student Guide p.8).

## Rubric

**AO2 Explore (15) + AO3 Develop (20) = 35 marks**, from Cambridge's Component 1 grid. Full grid: [[classes/a-level-art-design/9479-assessment-criteria|9479 Assessment Criteria]].

### AO2 Explore, 15 marks

*Explore and select appropriate resources, media, techniques and processes, reviewing and refining ideas as work develops.*

| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | 13–15 | Perceptive selection of media, techniques and processes · highly effective and **consistent** understanding of appropriate skills to explore visual elements · highly effective experimentation to review and refine ideas as work develops |
| **4** | 10–12 | Confident selection of appropriate media, techniques and processes · effective and consistent understanding of appropriate skills · effective experimentation to review and refine |
| **3** | 7–9 | Competent selection of media · competent understanding of appropriate skills · **relevant** experimentation to review and refine |
| **2** | 4–6 | Sufficient selection of media · adequate understanding of appropriate skills · adequate experimentation |
| **1** | 1–3 | Limited selection of media · basic understanding of appropriate skills · limited experimentation |
| **0** | 0 | No creditable response |

AO2 does **not** mean technical skill alone. Twelve beautiful experiments that were never reviewed or refined sit lower than eight rougher ones that visibly learned from each other.

### AO3 Develop, 20 marks

*Develop ideas through investigations informed by contextual and other sources, making purposeful connections and demonstrating analytical and critical understanding.*

| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **5** | 17–20 | **Perceptive and mature** connections between contextual and other sources · focused investigation demonstrating highly effective analytical and critical understanding · accomplished exploration of visual elements to communicate ideas · insightful reflection on own strengths and weaknesses |
| **4** | 13–16 | Confident connections · focused investigation demonstrating confident analytical and critical understanding · effective exploration of visual elements · effective reflection on own strengths and weaknesses |
| **3** | 9–12 | Competent connections · **thorough** investigation demonstrating competent analytical and critical understanding · competent exploration of visual elements · competent reflection |
| **2** | 5–8 | **Some evidence** of connections · adequate investigation demonstrating some analytical and critical understanding · adequate exploration · adequate reflection |
| **1** | 1–4 | Limited evidence of connections · limited investigation demonstrating basic analytical and critical understanding · limited exploration · basic reflection |
| **0** | 0 | No creditable response |

At this milestone AO3 is judged on the **connections**: research to recording to experiment to your own work. The bridge piece is where a marker looks for them. An artist page that describes an artist without changing anything downstream evidences AO3 at level 1 or 2 however well written it is.

Both marks are then curved under [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the BNDS framework]] rather than reported raw.
