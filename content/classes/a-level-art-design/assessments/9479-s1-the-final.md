## Objectives

The Final is not a task you prepare separately for. It asks one question: **taken as a whole, where is your portfolio against the Cambridge criteria at the end of Semester 1?**

What it establishes for you:

1. A single reading of your portfolio as one body of work rather than four milestones.
2. A Forecast Grade to carry into Semester 2 and the Personal Investigation.
3. A clear statement of which AO is currently your weakest, which is what Semester 2 planning acts on.

## The task

There is no task, and this is the important thing to understand about it.

A studio course has no exam paper to sit. Cambridge assesses Component 1 as one whole portfolio, once, at the end of the course. The school's End of Term Exam category asks for a summative 20% grade covering previously taught content. Both are satisfied by **assessing the portfolio you have already made**, against the same criteria Cambridge will use, at the semester boundary.

So there is nothing to revise for, nothing to bring, and nothing to hand in that you have not already handed in.

## Instructions

Submit A4 properly on Thu 2027-01-07 and the Final takes care of itself. It reads the same portfolio.

What happens:

1. Your A4 submission is marked against all four AOs (that is A4's own mark).
2. Your portfolio is then assessed **as a whole** against the Component 1 criteria, which is a different judgment: not the sum of four milestones but one reading of the body of work.
3. That whole-portfolio judgment is the End of Term grade, 20% of the semester.
4. It is reported with your other grades on Wed 2027-01-20.

**During the exam window (Mon 01-11 to Mon 01-18)** there are no Art and Design classes. You do not sit anything for this course that week.

## Rubric

The **same Cambridge Component 1 grid** used for A4, applied to the portfolio as a whole: [[classes/a-level-art-design/9479-assessment-criteria|9479 Assessment Criteria]].

| AO | Marks |
|---|---|
| AO1 Record | 15 |
| AO2 Explore | 15 |
| AO3 Develop | 20 |
| AO4 Present | 10 |
| **Total** | **60**, weighted to 90 by Cambridge |

The distinction between this and A4 is the unit of judgment, not the criteria:

- **A4** asks whether each AO is evidenced in the portfolio, milestone by milestone.
- **The Final** asks what level the portfolio sits at when read once, end to end, the way Cambridge will read it. A portfolio can pass every milestone and still read as level 3 overall if the parts do not add up to one coherent body of work.

Under [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the BNDS framework]] this grade is curved against CIE past-paper data allowing for where the class is in its learning trajectory, and it should make a reliable Forecast Grade. It is not a raw Cambridge score, and it is not a prediction of the mark Cambridge will award in 2028.

## After it

- Reported with grades and comments on **Wed 2027-01-20**.
- Read alongside your A4 feedback in the W21 one-to-ones, Tuesday to Thursday.
- The weakest-AO finding is what Semester 2 planning uses. Semester 2 is Component 3, the Personal Investigation, where AO1 and AO2 both carry more weight (27% each) than they do in Component 1.
