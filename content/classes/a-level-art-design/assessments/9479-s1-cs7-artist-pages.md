# A Level Art & Design · S1 CS7: Artist Pages

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Due | Fri 2026-10-30, written annotation |
| What | The Artist 1 pages, five parts |
| Test | The strike test |
| Feeds | A2 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Analyse an artist's method rather than their biography
2. Show an influence in your own making, not only in your writing
3. Write sentences that could only be about this artist

## Description

The Artist 1 pages, in five parts: **analysis, meaning and context, method study, personal response, influence note.**

Two things are marked above the rest.

**Does the influence show in your own work?** An influence note claiming you were changed, beside work that looks untouched, claims nothing.

**Does the writing survive the strike test?** Read each sentence and ask: could this describe any artist at all? If yes, strike it. "Uses bold colors to create emotion" describes several thousand artists. What is left after the striking is your actual analysis.

## Guidelines

- One artist, properly. Not three, skimmed.
- The method study is made, not described: you work their way to find out what it does.
- Personal response is your own piece, and it must show the influence.
- Run the strike test on yourself before handing in. Cross out the generic sentences.
- Name specifics: this work, this passage, this decision.
- Context means what made this work possible then. Not a life story.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | All five parts, and the influence is visible in the personal response without the note explaining it. Every surviving sentence is about this artist and no other. |
| **Secure** | Five parts present, influence traceable, and most sentences survive the strike test. |
| **Developing** | Parts present but the personal response could have been made without the artist, or much of the writing is generic praise. |
| **Not yet** | Missing parts, biography instead of analysis, or no method study made. |
