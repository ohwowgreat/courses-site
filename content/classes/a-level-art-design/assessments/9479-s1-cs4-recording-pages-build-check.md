# A Level Art & Design · S1 CS4: Recording Pages Build Check

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Due | Tue 2026-09-29, in the sketchbook |
| What | Recording pages started and sequenced |
| Why now | So A1's assembly is a check, not a scramble |
| Feeds | A1 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Sequence recording pages so a stranger reads them in order
2. Show intention, studies and reference as a path rather than a pile
3. Get ahead of an assembly deadline instead of meeting it at speed

## Description

Your recording pages, started and **sequenced so a stranger reads the recording in order**: intention, then studies, then reference.

The order is the assessment. A reader who does not know you should be able to open the sketchbook and follow how the work moved, without you standing beside it explaining.

This is checked before the break deliberately. When A1 comes, assembling it should be a check over work already in order, not a night of sorting.

## Guidelines

- Pages started, not finished. This is a build check.
- Put the intention where a reader meets it first.
- Studies follow the intention, and reference sits with the studies it belongs to.
- Number or otherwise mark the order if the sketchbook does not make it obvious.
- Ask someone in the class to read it cold and tell you the order they saw.
- Fix what they got wrong. That is the whole exercise.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A cold reader follows intention, studies and reference in the right order with no help. The sequence is a decision, and it shows. |
| **Secure** | The pages are started and broadly ordered; a reader needs one prompt to follow it. |
| **Developing** | Pages exist but sit in the order they happened rather than an order chosen. Reference is detached from what it informed. |
| **Not yet** | Little started, or no sequence discernible. |
