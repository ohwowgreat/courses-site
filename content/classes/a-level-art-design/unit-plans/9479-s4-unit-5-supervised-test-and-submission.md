# S4 Unit 5: Supervised Test & Submission

[[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-4-plan|Semester 4 Plan]] · Unit 5 of 5 · ← [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-s4-unit-4-development|Unit 4]]

## What this unit does

This is the test, assembly, and board stage, under **AO4 Present**. You realize your planned outcome inside the **15-hour supervised test** under exam conditions. Your work is stored securely between sittings: no access, no additions. You photograph the final piece and assemble the submission package exactly as the Cambridge specification orders it. Supervised quality control checks it line by line. On the linear entry, **C1, C2, and C3 all reach the board in the same window**. The unit closes with **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s4-assessments|A4]]**, the submission package as the internal grade layer. The final piece itself is marked by Cambridge.

## Dates & span

Weeks S7/S8–S10: the 15-hour test plus 10 sessions. The test runs in one of two forms, which will be confirmed in class:

- If printed (reading B): the test is in S8, Mon 2028-04-10 to Fri 04-14, 5 × 3h.
- If reading A: the test is in S7 around Qingming, 4 × 3h45, and the whole tail moves forward one week.

All S4 dates are projected and may shift when the school publishes the calendar. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-4-plan|semester plan]].
