This is the final-outcome and portfolio stage, under **AO4 Present**. You build the final outcome in one protected continuous run: three build days, a mid-production review in the Friday double whose second period carries out the top-ranked changes, then a two-day resolve run. After that, submission week treats assembly as a supervised craft: examiner-grade photography, sequencing the whole journey, an edit with a cut log, and [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a4-final-portfolio|A4]], the final portfolio, marked across AO1–AO4. This closes the semester.

## Dates & span

Mon 2026-12-14 → Fri 2027-01-08 (W16 → W19), with the winter break (W18) between the declared finish and supervised assembly. The late-work cutoff (about Fri 2027-01-01) falls during the break.

## Assessment

**Full briefs** (objectives, task, instructions, rubric): [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a4-final-portfolio|A4 Final Portfolio]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-the-final|The Final]].

You sit **A4 on Thu 2027-01-07**, the final portfolio: the resolved outcome plus the complete supporting portfolio, sequenced and edited, marked against AO1–AO4. No edits after submission. A4 is announced Mon 2026-12-21, a 17-day window that covers the break. Grades and comments are due Wed 2027-01-20. One CS task runs in this unit: **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS10]]** (mid-production review reflection), Fri 2026-12-18, which is direct practice for A4. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- The build run is the semester's longest, and it is protected. Any lost day here has no home elsewhere: the calendar has zero spare days. Nothing new enters the room during the run. If you deviate from the plan, that is a conference, not a drift.
- The **declared finish** is Tue 2026-12-22. 9479 has no Wednesday session, so Tuesday is the last session before the break. This finish is real: the break is rest, with no homework, and you assemble the portfolio only in the supervised January sessions.
- Submission craft: the photograph is the version the examiner sees. The sequence reads theme, then recording, then exploration, then artists, then development, then outcome, with no narration. You cut at least one page that does not earn its place and log the cut. That cut log is your evidence of editorial judgment if a mark is queried.
- Friday closes with the **C3 bridge**. Your winter homework is three candidate Personal Investigation themes with annotated references. This opens the Semester 2 consultation cycle on Mon 2027-02-22.
