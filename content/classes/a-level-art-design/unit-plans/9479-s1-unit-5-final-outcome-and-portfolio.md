# S1 Unit 5: Final Outcome & Portfolio

[[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 5 of 5 · ← [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-s1-unit-4-development|Unit 4]]

## What this unit does

This is the final-outcome and portfolio stage, under **AO4 Present**. You build the final outcome in one protected run: four build days, a mid-production review with real time still left, then three resolve days, all set out in the Lesson 14 plan. Submission week treats assembly as a supervised craft. You photograph the work at examiner grade, sequence the whole journey, edit it with a cut log, and submit **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a4-final-portfolio|A4]]**, the final portfolio. A4 is marked across AO1–AO4 and closes the semester.

## Dates & span

Mon 2026-12-14 → Fri 2027-01-08 (W16 → W19). The winter break (W18) falls between the declared finish and supervised assembly. The late-work cutoff (about Fri 2027-01-01) passes during the break. These dates may shift when the school publishes the calendar for that period.

## Lessons

- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-14-the-final-outcome|Lesson 14: The Final Outcome]] — four-day build (underdrawing, mid-stages, production, detail), a mid-production review while change is still possible, then a three-day resolve run ending in a written declared finish.
- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-15-submission-week-and-a4|Lesson 15: Submission Week and A4]] — photograph, sequence, edit with cut log, final AO check, submit on Thursday, then semester reflection and the C3 bridge on Friday.

## Assessment

**Full briefs** (objectives, task, instructions, rubric): [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a4-final-portfolio|A4 Final Portfolio]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-the-final|The Final]].

You sit **A4 on Thu 2027-01-07**: the final portfolio, which is the resolved outcome plus the complete supporting portfolio, sequenced and edited. It is marked against AO1–AO4. You cannot edit after you submit. A4 is announced on Mon 2026-12-21, a 17-day window that covers the break. Grades and comments are returned by Wed 2027-01-20. One CS task supports this unit: **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS10]]**, the mid-production review reflection, due Fri 2026-12-18. CS10 is direct practice for A4. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- The build run is the semester's longest, and it is protected. Any day you lose here has no home elsewhere, because the calendar has zero spare days. Nothing new enters the room during the run. If you need to change course, you have a conference, you do not drift from the plan.
- The declared finish (Wed 2026-12-23) is real. The break is rest with no homework. You assemble the portfolio only in the supervised January sessions.
- Submission craft: the photograph is the version the examiner sees. The sequence reads theme, recording, exploration, artists, development, outcome, with no narration. You cut at least one page that does not earn its place, and you log the cut. The cut log is your evidence of editorial judgment if a mark is queried.
- Friday closes with the C3 bridge. Winter homework is three candidate Personal Investigation themes with annotated references. This opens the Semester 2 consultation cycle on Mon 2027-02-22.
