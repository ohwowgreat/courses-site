This is the recording and sustained-study stage of the pipeline. It sits entirely under **AO1 Record**. You turn the theme into a written investigation: an intention, a mind map, and thumbnails with annotated rejections. You give it a spine: one long observed study carried across the week without restarting. Everything you record becomes pages an examiner could read. The unit closes with **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a1-recording-milestone|A1]]**, the semester's first attainment and your first use of the **attainment review protocol**.

## Dates & span

Tue 2026-09-15 to Sat 2026-10-10 (W3 to W6), split by the National Day break. The Sun 2026-09-20 and Sat 2026-10-10 make-up days carry sessions only if their timetables include a 9479 day; this will be confirmed in class. Sat 2026-10-10 also has Sports Day in the afternoon. These dates may shift when the school publishes the calendar for this period.

## Assessment

**Full brief** (objectives, task, instructions, rubric): [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a1-recording-milestone|A1 Recording]].

You sit **A1 on Fri 2026-10-09**, the recording milestone (intention, Stage 1 pages, sustained study, supports), marked against AO1. It feeds the mid-term grades due Thu 2026-10-29. The CS tasks inside the unit are: **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS2]]** Fri 2026-09-18 (intention + mind map), **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS3]]** Thu 2026-09-24 (sustained-study walked check), and **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS4]]** Tue 2026-09-29 (recording pages build check). Each is direct practice for A1. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- Your sustained-study media are assigned to you from the W1 diagnostic.
- The **intention drafting frame** (*I am investigating / because / I will record*) helps you start in clear English, then is dropped so the voice ends up your own.
- The AO1 self-check before A1 builds your calibration habit: your own banding should land within one band of the teacher's. Self-bandings are collected and the gap tracked.
- The holiday split is deliberate: you sequence and start your pages before the break (Tue 09-29, with CS4), then finish them with the self-check after it (Thu 10-08). Holiday homework is primary-source sketchbook recording.
