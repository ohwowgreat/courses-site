# S1 Unit 4: Development

[[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 4 of 5 · ← [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-s1-unit-3-exploration-and-artist-study|Unit 3]] | [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-s1-unit-5-final-outcome-and-portfolio|Unit 5]] →

## What this unit does

This is the development stage, assessed under AO3 Develop. You build two full compositions — first your chosen direction, then a genuine alternative you reject — and critique both against your intention. Targeted tests answer only what the critique left unresolved. The unit ends by planning your final outcome completely, then reviewing the whole development trail at A3. AO3 closes into AO4 in the final lesson.

## Dates & span

Wed 2026-11-11 → Fri 2026-12-11 (W11 → W15), around the second G11 Juniors Days block (Wed–Fri of W14).

## Lessons

- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-10-composition-1|Lesson 10: Composition 1]] — composed from your own studies using tracing-paper overlays, built over two days, resolved and honestly photographed.
- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-11-composition-2-and-critique|Lesson 11: Composition 2 and Critique]] — the rejected alternative, built deliberately faster; a full-protocol structured critique; you change something because of what you heard.
- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-12-targeted-tests-and-direction|Lesson 12: Targeted Tests and Direction]] — targeted tests, not decoration; final direction with your rejection written in full sentences; a written evaluation; the Stage 6 page as a decision trail.
- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-13-final-plan-and-a3|Lesson 13: Final Plan and A3]] — final composition plan, solving thumbnails, palette argued from meaning; you sit A3; you amend the plan from feedback.

Two CS tasks give you direct practice for A3: **CS8** on Fri 2026-11-20 (both compositions photographed and laid out) and **CS9** on Fri 2026-11-27 (targeted tests + critique response). See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- Keep the one-sentence direction from A2 at your desk. If your development drifts from it, you will be conferenced the same day.
- Two-day builds never restart — carrying a single piece through is the point. Composition 2's one-day build is intentional pressure: the process moves faster the second time.
- The rejection paragraph (final direction, Fri of Lesson 12) is the single highest-leverage piece of writing in your AO4 story.
- Your final plan must be clear enough that a capable stranger could begin the piece from it. Choose palette and materials for meaning, and name why.
- Documentation is taught here because it pays at submission: a bad photograph loses real marks.
