This is the development stage, under **AO3 Develop**. You build two full compositions: first your chosen direction, then a genuine alternative you go on to reject. You critique both against your intention. You run targeted tests that answer only what the critique left open. You finish by planning the final outcome in full. [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a3-development-milestone|A3]] then reviews the whole development trail. In the last lesson, AO3 closes into **AO4**.

## Dates & span

Thu 2026-11-12 to Fri 2026-12-11 (W11 to W15). 9479 has no Wednesday session. This unit runs around the second G11 Juniors Days block (Wed to Fri of W14). These dates may shift when the school publishes the calendar for that period.

## Assessment

**Full brief** (objectives, task, instructions, rubric): [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a3-development-milestone|A3 Development]].

This unit gates on **A3, Thu 2026-12-10**, the development milestone. A3 marks compositions 1 and 2, your targeted tests, your written evaluation, and your final composition plan against AO3. CS tasks feed directly into A3: **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS8]]** Fri 2026-11-20 (both compositions photographed and laid out) and **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS9]]** Fri 2026-11-27 (targeted tests plus critique response). See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- Keep your one-sentence direction from [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a2-exploration-and-artist-study|A2]] at your desk. If your development drifts from it, you talk it through the same day.
- Two-day builds never restart. Carrying a piece across days is the point. Composition 2 gets one day on purpose: the process moves faster the second time.
- The rejection paragraph in your final direction (Fri of Lesson 12) is the strongest single piece of writing in your AO4 story.
- Aim your final plan so that a capable stranger could begin the piece from it. Choose your palette and materials for meaning, and name why.
- Documentation is taught here because it counts at submission. A bad photograph loses real marks.
