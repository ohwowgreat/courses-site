# S1 Unit 1: Foundations on the Theme

[[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 1 of 5 · [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-s1-unit-2-recording-and-sustained-study|Unit 2]] →

## What this unit does

This unit opens the Component 1 portfolio at its foundations stage. You learn how the portfolio is marked by the four **Assessment Objectives (AOs)**, what a top-band portfolio does, and you make the semester's biggest decision: you commit to one theme from the menu against set criteria, not by mood. You then work the **formal elements** directly on your chosen theme, with a focus on AO1 Record. By the unit's close, you have looked at the theme through five instruments, and your first portfolio page can argue something.

## Dates & span

Tue 2026-09-01 → Mon 2026-09-14 (W1 → W3 Monday; 10 sessions). No grades count in W1–W2. Your day-3 diagnostic drawing is the baseline for the mid-term.

## Lessons

- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-01-introduction-and-the-theme-menu|Lesson 01: Introduction and the Theme Menu]] — 96/100 exemplar briefing, the four AOs, diagnostic drawing, theme committed Thursday against five criteria, first sources and line studies.
- [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s1-lesson-02-formal-elements-on-the-theme|Lesson 02: Formal Elements on the Theme]] — line, tone builds form, push to fail, matching mark to surface, color from observation; Recording Page 1 ("a page that argues") and digital portfolio setup. Ends the unit.

## Assessment

No grades gate this unit. It holds one CS task: **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-course-skills|CS1]]** (formal elements study set plus Recording Page 1, in your sketchbook), collected Mon 2026-09-14. It is practice toward [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-a1-recording-milestone|A1]]. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- **The theme menu:** Reflections and Surfaces · Worn and Weathered · Hands at Work · Structures of the Everyday. The final menu will be confirmed in class. Your theme must pass all five criteria: photographable primary sources within daily reach; survives four months of looking; carries line, tone, texture, and color; opens both 2D and 3D; has a deep pool of artist references to draw from. Sign-off is one-to-one, and the commitment card is physical.
- **Source rule:** "Nothing found, nothing borrowed." Every source is your own photograph or observation.
- **Studio routines:** the session shape (entry look → demo → sustained work with a midpoint checkpoint → exit annotation); the annotation rule (record decisions, rejections, and observations, not feelings); the homework rule (sketchbook, annotation, or reflection only, 30 minutes or less).
