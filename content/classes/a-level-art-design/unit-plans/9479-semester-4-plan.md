# A Level Art & Design · Semester 4 Plan

> [[classes/a-level-art-design/a-level-art-design|A Level Art & Design]] · Semester 4 of 4 ([[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-course-map|course map]]) · ← [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-3-plan|S3 C3 part 2]] · course closes

**Component 2 (the Externally Set Assignment) plus the submission of all components, G12, spring 2027-28.** You have one session every teaching day, from Mon 2028-02-21 to the content close on Fri 2028-03-31, then the mock window, the 15-hour supervised test, assembly, and the board handover: **40 coded sessions** (30 in the six-week core, 10 in assembly and board) plus the elastic window and the test. The eight-stage pipeline is compressed onto the Cambridge set question.

**Dates.** All dates here are projected. They may shift when the school publishes the spring 2027-28 calendar. A4 and the test move with the mock window.
