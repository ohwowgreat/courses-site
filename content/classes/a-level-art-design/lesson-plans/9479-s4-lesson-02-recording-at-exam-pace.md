## The ideas

### Exam pace

**Exam pace** means you work faster, but the rules do not relax. Speed does not excuse borrowed reference, unbuilt pages, or extra homework.

### Self-generated reference

**Self-generated reference** is reference you make yourself: you shoot it and draw it. The rule is "nothing found, nothing borrowed," and it does not relax under exam pressure. You state your intention and plan the shoot in the same session, then shoot and draw the reference within the same week.

### Sustained study

A **sustained study** is one observed study that you open now and keep developing across the unit. You do not restart it next session. You push it past comfortable next week.

### Recording pages

**Recording pages** are built as the work happens. In an exam-paced unit there is no later. You annotate every page back to your locked question, saying what each part gives the question.

### Homework cap

Homework stays capped at 30 minutes. Exam pace is not exam-season homework inflation.

## Day by day

- **Mon 02-28.** State your intention on the locked question and plan the reference shoot in the same session.
- **Tue–Wed 02-29/03-01.** Studio: shoot your primary source and make first studies over a 2-day run. Reference is generated, not sourced.
- **Thu 03-02.** Open your sustained study, to develop across the unit, not restart next session.
- **Fri 03-03.** Build recording pages as the work happens. Submit **CS2**: intention, thumbnails, and shoot plan. Homework (≤30 min): annotate the shoot, saying what each frame gives the question.

## Assessment

**CS2** is graded on Fri 2028-03-03: your intention, thumbnails, and shoot plan. There is no attainment grade this week.

## Review

Check you can:

- State an intention on your locked question and plan the reference shoot in the same session.
- Explain why all reference is **self-generated reference**: nothing found, nothing borrowed.
- Shoot and draw your reference within the same week.
- Open a **sustained study** and keep developing it instead of restarting.
- Build **recording pages** as the work happens and annotate each back to the question.
