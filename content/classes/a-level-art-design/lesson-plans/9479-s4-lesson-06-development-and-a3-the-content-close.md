## Overview

This is the tightest week in the unit, and the point after which nothing new is taught. You compose your final-piece candidates from your own studies, compare them on a draft comparison page with the rejections written out, and fix a final composition plan so the supervised test is execution, not invention. You submit **CS6** on Thu 2028-03-30 and sit **A3** on Fri 2028-03-31, which is the content close. For homework (up to 30 minutes), write the rejection for the candidate you set aside: why it loses to the one you kept. ## The ideas

### Composing and choosing

**Final-piece candidates** are the final composition options you build from your own studies. The supervised test will realize one of them, so they must be real, not sketches you cannot execute.

The **draft comparison page** is where you set the candidates against each other and write out your rejections: why the one you kept beats the one you set aside. This page is the center of gravity for the unit. In the exemplars, a comparison page with the rejections written out earns top AO4.

### Fixing the plan

The **final composition plan** must be complete enough that the supervised test becomes execution, not invention. Nothing new is invented once you are under supervision. Alongside it, every supporting study is completed and the mounted order is decided.

### The content close

**[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s4-assessments|A3]] is the content close.** Nothing new is taught after A3. The compression here is deliberate: there is one taught development week, widened only by the elastic window (reading B), and every gained day lands in this unit. A3 is marked across AO3 and AO4.

## Day by day

- **Mon 03-27.** Feedback, then compose your final-piece candidates from your own studies.
- **Tue 03-28.** Build the draft comparison page: compare the candidates with the rejections written out.
- **Wed 03-29.** Fix the final composition plan, complete enough that the test is execution, not invention.
- **Thu 03-30.** Complete every supporting study and close any gaps. Submit **[[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s4-assessments|CS6]]**: the composition critique response plus the draft comparison page.
- **Fri 03-31.** Sit **A3**, marked across AO3 and AO4: comparison page, final plan, and mounted order. This is the content close, and nothing new is taught after today.

## Review

Check you can:

- Compose final-piece candidates from your own studies.
- Build a draft comparison page and write out your rejections in words.
- Fix a final composition plan complete enough that the test is execution, not invention.
- Explain why A3 is the content close and what it means that nothing new is taught after it.

## Practice

Try each question before you open its answer. After Friday, nothing new is taught. Arrive knowing why.

**1.** Why must the final-piece candidates be composed from your own studies?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Because the supervised test realizes one of them. A candidate composed from nowhere cannot be executed from preparation, and the whole component is marked on how the test piece grows from the supporting studies. The candidates must be real enough to build from.

</details>

**2.** What is the draft comparison page, and what do the exemplars say about it?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

The candidates compared side by side with the rejections articulated in writing: why the kept one wins, why each other one loses. This page earns top AO4 in the exemplars. It is the unit's center of gravity because it shows the final piece was chosen, not defaulted to.

</details>

**3.** State the bar the final composition plan must clear.

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Complete enough that the supervised test is execution, not invention. Fifteen hours under supervision is enough time to build a planned piece and nowhere near enough to design one. Every decision left open is a decision made in the worst room for it.

</details>

**4.** What does "content closes at A3" mean?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Nothing new is taught after Friday. The compression is deliberate: one taught development week, widened only by the elastic window, with every gained day landing here. A3 marks the comparison page, the final plan, the mounted order, and the completed supporting studies across AO3 and AO4.

</details>

**5.** The homework is the rejection for the candidate you set aside: why it loses to the one you kept, in full sentences. You have written this paragraph in every component. This is the last time it decides a real piece, so write it like it matters, because it does.
