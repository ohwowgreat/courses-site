## Overview

This week you turn your exploration into a commitment. You critique the experiment grids, select your media for reasons you can write down, then test that choice at real scale. You produce a Stage 3 page and a media selection note, collected as **CS6** on Fri 2026-10-23. This is direct practice for A2.

## The ideas

The AO focus this week is AO2 Explore.

### Group critique

**Group critique** is a shared review of the exploration grids using a set protocol. The maker speaks last, so the work is discussed before the maker defends it. Log the two comments that changed your view.

### Media selection

The **selection note** is a written annotation that earns your choice. It names your chosen media in AO language: what this medium does to this theme. Mount your chosen strips beside the note.

### Scale trial and refinement

The **scale trial** tests your chosen media at real size on your chosen composition. Work at outcome scale, then annotate what scale broke. **Refinement** means you fix what broke, nothing else, and annotate the fix.

### The Stage 3 page

The **Stage 3 page** shows exploration as reasoning, not as a swatch chart. Lay it out in this order: experiments, decision, trial, refinement.

## Day by day

- **Mon 10-19.** Grids go up for a group critique of the exploration; the maker speaks last. Log the two comments that changed your view.
- **Tue 10-20.** Select your media and document it in a selection note, with your chosen strips mounted beside the note. One-to-one sign-offs begin.
- **Wed 10-21.** Run the scale trial: chosen media at real size on the chosen composition. Annotate what scale broke.
- **Thu 10-22.** Refine: fix what broke, nothing else, and annotate the fix.
- **Fri 10-23.** Lay out the Stage 3 page as reasoning, in order: experiments, decision, trial, refinement. **CS6 collected** with the selection note.

## Assessment

**CS6** (Stage 3 page + media selection note, written annotation) collects Fri 2026-10-23. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]]. The selection note names your chosen media in AO language: what this medium does to this theme. The Autumn Field Trip falls on Fri 10-23 or Sat 10-24; if it takes Friday, CS6 collects Monday. The exact day will be confirmed in class.

## Review

Check you can:

- Select media and give written reasons for the choice in a selection note.
- Name your chosen media in AO language: what this medium does to this theme.
- Test your chosen media at real scale and annotate what the scale trial broke.
- Refine the work to fix what broke, and annotate the fix.
- Lay out the Stage 3 page so it reads as reasoning: experiments, decision, trial, refinement.
