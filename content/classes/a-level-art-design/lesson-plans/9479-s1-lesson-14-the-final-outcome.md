## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Build the final outcome in one sustained run from underdrawing to surface
2. Act on the midpoint hard look while change is still possible
3. Resolve the focal areas and unify the whole surface

## Goal

The real thing, in one continuous run: underdrawing through to surface across four days, a hard look on Friday while there is still time to change things, then three days to resolve the focal areas and unify the whole. The plan from Lesson 13 governs every session; if you want to depart from it, discuss it in a conference first rather than drifting.

## How it runs

- **Mon 12-14 → Thu 12-17** — four-day build run, one continuous session: day 1 underdrawing and block-in; day 2 mid-stages (main relationships established); day 3 production; day 4 detail and surface. Each day: re-entry look, build, exit annotation against the plan.
- **Fri 12-18** — mid-production review: your piece and your plan side by side, teacher and peer pass, protocol applied, changes listed and ranked. **CS10**: write and hand in the review reflection — what changes, in what order, and why.
- **Mon 12-21 → Wed 12-23** — three-day resolve run (criteria read, date on the board: Thu 2027-01-07): day 6 focal areas; day 7 surface unity; day 8 final touches and stop — you declare the piece finished in writing.
