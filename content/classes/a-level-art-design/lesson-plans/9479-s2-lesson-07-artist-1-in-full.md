## Overview

This week Unit 3 turns from your own experiments to one artist who solves the problem you are working on. You choose and analyze Artist 1, write their meaning and context, study their method by copying, and answer them with a personal response. Your deliverable is **CS5**, the Artist 1 pages (analysis, method, response), due Fri 2027-04-16. The context you write here becomes the first material of your research paper. These dates fall in the 2026–27 school year and may shift once the school publishes the calendar for that period.

## The ideas

### Choosing Artist 1

Choose **Artist 1** for the problem they solve, not because you like them. Pick an artist whose work answers a problem that is live in your investigation: a composition, a process, or a way with the material. Then analyze how the work is made and why it holds, using specialist language.

### Meaning and context

Place the artist. Say what the work meant, when it was made, and for whom. This is the **first material of your research paper**: write it properly so it gathers into the written element from the start, not as a detached bibliography later.

### Method study

Do a **method study**: copy a passage of the work to understand how it was made. The rule is **copy to understand, not to own**. Note what the copying taught you that looking alone could not.

### Personal response

Answer the artist with a **personal response** built from your own sources. Take the method and turn it on your theme. **Respond, do not imitate**: a copy with your subject placed on top is not a response.

### The influence page

Lay out the artist page so the influence is visible in your own work, not just described. The reader should see the artist's effect in what you make.
