## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Compose from your own recorded studies rather than from memory
2. Build across two uninterrupted days and resolve the piece
3. Photograph the work to a standard that does not lose marks

## Goal

Development begins with making, not talking. You compose Composition 1 from your own recorded studies, never from memory, then build it across two uninterrupted days, resolve it, and document it properly. Unresolved work cannot be judged, and badly photographed work loses real marks.

## How it runs

- **Wed 11-11** — composing from studies: tracing-paper overlays, the direction sentence taped to the board edge; compose Composition 1 as a full-size cartoon or worked drawing; sign-off rota begins.
- **Thu 11-12 → Fri 11-13** — two-day build run: Thursday blocks structure and main relationships; Friday develops passages toward the whole; write an exit annotation each day — the decision that held the idea together.
- **Mon 11-16** — name the unresolved passages, then resolve Composition 1: finish it, and declare it done in writing.
- **Tue 11-17** — documenting work: light, angle, color truth; photograph and lay out Composition 1 with its trail (studies, cartoon, build shots); file it to the digital portfolio.
