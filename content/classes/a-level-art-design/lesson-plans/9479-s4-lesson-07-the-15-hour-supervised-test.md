## Overview

This is the exam proper. You realise your planned final piece inside the **15-hour supervised test** under exam conditions. The days before the test are for refinement and rehearsal only, not new work. Your deliverable is the finished final piece, made under supervision, then stored securely between sittings. Assembly of the submission package follows in [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s4-lesson-08-assembly-and-a4-the-submission-package|Lesson 08]].

## The ideas

### Nothing invented under supervision

**Nothing is invented under supervision.** Content closed at A3, so the plan you marked there is what you make. No additions, no new ideas under exam conditions. That plan is both your safety and your mark.

### Secure storage between sittings

Between sittings, your work is stored securely and documented. There is no access, no additions, and no exceptions. The integrity of the test depends on this.

### The test floats

**The test floats.** It fits only the mock window or a school exam window, never the daily timetable. This is why the tail of the unit is planned two ways. On one reading the test runs Mon 2028-04-10 → Fri 04-14 in five sittings of 3 hours. On the other reading it runs around Qingming (Tue 04-04) in four sittings of 3h45, and the whole unit, the test, U5, A4, and CS7, pulls forward one week.

## Day by day

- **The elastic window.** The teaching days of the mock-window week go to refinement and rehearsal of your plan, not new work. On the earlier reading these days are absorbed and everything moves forward one week.
- **The supervised test (Reading B).** Mon 2028-04-10 → Fri 04-14, five sittings of 3 hours. You realise the plan under exam conditions.
- **The supervised test (Reading A).** Around Qingming (Tue 04-04), four sittings of 3h45.
- These dates are projected. They may shift when the school publishes the exam calendar, and the final sitting dates will be confirmed in class. No homework: rest between sittings.

## Assessment

The 15-hour supervised test is graded. It is marked externally by Cambridge. There is no CS or attainment mark this block.

## Review

Check you can:

- Realise your planned final piece inside the supervised test under exam conditions.
- Explain why **nothing is invented under supervision**, because content closed at A3.
