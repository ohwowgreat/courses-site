## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Realise your planned final piece inside the supervised test under exam conditions
2. Invent nothing under supervision, because your content closed at A3
3. Use the elastic days before the test for refinement and rehearsal only

## Goal

This is the exam proper. The outcome you planned at A3 is **realised inside the 15-hour supervised test** under exam conditions — no additions, no access between sittings, **nothing invented under supervision**. The elastic days before the test go to refinement and rehearsal, never to new content, because your content closed at A3.

## How it runs

- **The elastic window.** The teaching days before the test go to **refinement and rehearsal** of your plan — not new work.
- **The 15-hour supervised test.** Your plan realised under exam conditions. The test runs Mon 2028-04-10 → Fri 04-14, five sittings of 3 hours. In the alternative form it runs around Qingming (Tue 04-04) as four sittings of 3h45.
- **Between sittings — secure storage.** Work is stored securely and documented: **no access, no additions, no exceptions.**

## Key content: nothing invented under supervision

- ◆ **Nothing is invented under supervision** — the plan you marked at A3 is both your safety and your mark.
- Storage between sittings is **secure and documented**: the integrity of the test depends on it.
- If the test runs in the earlier form, the whole unit — test, U5, A4, CS7 — moves one week earlier. Assembly of the submission package follows in [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s4-lesson-08-assembly-and-a4-the-submission-package|L08]].
