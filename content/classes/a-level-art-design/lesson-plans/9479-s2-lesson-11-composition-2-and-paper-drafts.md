## Overview

This week you give the idea you rejected a fair trial. You compose, build, and resolve **composition 2**, and you draft your research paper sections against your outline. On Fri 2027-05-14 you hand in **CS7**, the research paper section drafts. Both finished compositions feed next week's critique, and the paper drafting builds toward **A3**, which sits Fri 2027-05-28.

## The ideas

### Composition 2 and equal effort


<figure class="plate">
  <img src="../../../static/img/vanitas.jpg" alt="Edwaert Collier, Vanitas Still Life, 1662" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Edwaert Collier, <em>Vanitas Still Life</em>, 1662</figcaption>
</figure>

**Composition 2** is the alternative you set aside when you chose composition 1. You build it with **equal effort**: the same commitment, the same two-day build, and the same resolved standard as the first. It is not a token second attempt.

A fair comparison needs two finished things, not one finished and one sketched. If you under-build composition 2, the comparison is rigged and the development reads as a foregone conclusion. Built honestly, composition 2 gives the rejected idea a real chance, so next week's critique tests a genuine choice.

### Integrated research paper writing

You move the research paper from outline to prose. You draft each section against the structure you populated, turning artist context and argument into real sentences. The aim is **integrated writing**: prose built from your own material, tied to the visual work rather than detached from it. Drafting section by section keeps the writing in step with the making, so it does not pile up at the end.

## Day by day

- **Mon 05-10.** Compose composition 2, the alternative you rejected when you chose composition 1.
- **Tue 05-11.** Build composition 2, day 1, with the same effort you gave the first.
- **Wed 05-12.** Build composition 2, day 2. Carry it to the same depth.
- **Thu 05-13.** Resolve composition 2 to the same standard as composition 1.
- **Fri 05-14.** Draft your research paper sections against your outline. **CS7** collects the section drafts. Homework (≤30 min): annotate toward next week's critique and bring both compositions ready to compare.

## Assessment

**CS7** (research paper section drafts) is due **Fri 2027-05-14**, a written annotation marked on whether the sections are real prose built from your own material, integrated with the visual work rather than detached. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s2-assessments|full register]]. With both compositions resolved, next week is the critique in [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s2-lesson-12-critique-and-targeted-tests|Lesson 12]]: the two set against your intention, targeted tests on what is still unresolved, and the final direction chosen. **A3** (development + research paper draft 1, AO3) sits **Fri 2027-05-28**.

## Review

Check you can:

- Compose the alternative you set aside as **composition 2**.
- Build and resolve composition 2 with **equal effort**, to the same standard as composition 1.
- Explain why a fair comparison needs two finished compositions, not one finished and one sketched.
- Draft your research paper sections as **integrated writing**, real prose built from your own material and tied to the visual work.
