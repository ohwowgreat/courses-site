## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Read the Cambridge paper closely and name what each question permits and quietly forbids
2. Match your choice to proven strengths from C1 and C3, evidenced rather than hoped
3. Lock one question in writing with a rationale

## Goal

Component 2 opens on the Cambridge paper itself. **This is the exam.** By Friday you will have read the paper closely and named each question for what it permits and quietly forbids, matched your choice to **proven strengths from C1 and C3** — shown by your portfolio and drawing regimen, not by hope — and locked one question **in writing with a rationale**. The whole semester runs on this decision, so make it against evidence.

## How it runs

- **Mon 02-21 — course reopening and the ESA briefing.** You read the Cambridge paper together: what the ESA is, how it is marked, and the shape of the spring.
- **Tue 02-22 — question analysis.** What each question permits, and what it quietly forbids — the constraints that decide feasibility.
- **Wed 02-23 — strengths audit.** Match questions to proven strengths from C1 and C3, not to preference — the exam rewards what you can already do at pace.
- **Thu 02-24 — question shortlist, one to one.** Bring two candidate questions and defend them against the evidence.
- **Fri 02-25 — question locked with rationale; CS1.** You lock your question in writing. CS1 is that choice with its rationale. **A1** is announced today, exactly 14 days out.

## Key content: choose on evidence, then commit

- The strengths audit runs on **what your portfolio shows, not on preference** — the exam rewards what you can already do at pace.
- You lock the question in writing by Friday with a rationale grounded in evidence; there is no restart after the lock.
- The winter regimen closes here — your past-paper review notes and drawing-regimen evidence feed the audit directly.
- Recording at exam pace begins in [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s4-lesson-02-recording-at-exam-pace|L02]].
