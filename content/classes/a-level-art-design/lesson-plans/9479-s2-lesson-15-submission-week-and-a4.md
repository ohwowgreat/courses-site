## Overview

This week you submit the **mid-point portfolio** and sit A4. Over five days you photograph the work, sequence the whole journey, edit it, pass supervised quality control, and submit on Friday. The deliverable is the submitted portfolio and the A4 mark against AO1–AO4, due Fri 2027-06-11, with no edits after. Your work then feeds the Final in S17 (Mon 2027-06-13 → 19), where the End-of-Term category (20%) is met by a teacher-marked assessment of your portfolio-so-far against the Cambridge criteria: no exam paper, no invigilation, no new task, and your mid-point portfolio is returned one to one. The Final's format will be confirmed in class. It also feeds the summer production briefing in S18 (Mon 2027-06-20 → 26), which sets teacher targets piece by piece, the largest uninterrupted C3 window before the investigation resumes in [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-3-plan|Semester 3]] (autumn 2027, C3 part 2). These spring and summer dates are provisional and may shift when the school publishes the calendar for that period.

## The ideas

### The photograph is what the examiner sees

The **photograph is the version the examiner sees**. The examiner marks from your images, not the physical work, so the shoot decides the mark. Rig, light, and shoot the work properly, with detail passes on the resolved piece. Then crop, color-correct, name and file every image to the template.

### Sequencing the journey

Sequencing means laying out the whole journey in order: theme, recording, exploration, artists, development, resolved piece, paper draft. The test is that a stranger reads it without narration. Nothing should need you to explain it.

### Editing and the AO check

Editing is cutting what does not earn its place, and logging every cut. After cutting, check all four AOs in writing: recording, exploration, development, presentation.

### Supervised quality control

Quality control is a supervised, page-by-page check against the checklist: images clean, bibliography integrated, paper draft in place, sequence reading. You make fixes only. Nothing new starts, and there are no edits after submission.

## Day by day

- **Mon 06-07.** Photograph and prepare images: rig, light, shoot, run detail passes on the resolved piece, then crop, correct, name and file to the template.
- **Tue 06-08.** Sequence the mid-point portfolio so the whole journey reads in order without narration.
- **Wed 06-09.** Edit: cut what does not earn its place, log every cut, then check all four AOs in writing.
- **Thu 06-10.** Supervised final quality control, page by page against the checklist. Fixes only.
- **Fri 06-11.** A4: the mid-point portfolio is due and submitted in session, marked against AO1–AO4. No edits after. Homework after A4 is the semester reflection.

## Assessment

You sit **A4** (mid-point portfolio) on **Fri 2027-06-11**, marked against AO1–AO4. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s2-assessments|full register]]. It is the whole investigation so far, sequenced and edited: recording, exploration, artist pages, development, one resolved piece and the research paper draft. The sequence must read the whole journey in order without narration. There are no edits after submission.

## Review

Check you can:

- Photograph your work so the images are the version the examiner sees.
- Sequence the mid-point portfolio so a stranger reads the whole journey without narration.
- Cut what does not earn its place and log every cut.
- Check all four AOs in writing: recording, exploration, development, presentation.
- Pass supervised quality control and submit A4 with no edits after.
