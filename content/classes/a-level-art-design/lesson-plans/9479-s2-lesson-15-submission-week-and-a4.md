## Overview

This is submission week for the mid-point portfolio. Across the week you photograph, sequence, edit, and pass supervised quality control on everything from the locked theme to the resolved piece and the research paper draft. Treat it as a supervised craft, not a scramble. You submit at [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s2-assessments|A4]] on Fri 2027-06-11, marked against AO1–AO4, with no edits after. After A4 you write the semester reflection. The summer production briefing then sets your targets for the summer, the largest uninterrupted C3 window before the investigation resumes in Semester 3.

## The ideas

### Photographing the work

**The photograph is the version the examiner sees.** You do not submit the physical work; you submit its images. So rig, light, and shoot the work carefully, with detail passes on the resolved piece. Then crop, correct, name, and file each image to the template.

### Sequencing the mid-point portfolio

Sequencing means laying out the whole journey in order: theme, recording, exploration, artists, development, resolved piece, and research paper draft. A stranger should be able to read it in order without narration.

### Editing and the AO check

Editing means you cut what does not earn its place, and you log every cut. Then you check all four Assessment Objectives (AO1–AO4) in writing: recording, exploration, development, and presentation.

### Supervised quality control

**Quality control (QC)** is a page-by-page pass against the checklist: images clean, bibliography integrated, paper draft in place, sequence reading. During QC you make fixes only. Nothing new starts.

## Day by day

- **Mon 06-07.** Photograph and prepare the images: rig, light, and shoot, with detail passes on the resolved piece, then crop, correct, name, and file to the template.
- **Tue 06-08.** Sequence the mid-point portfolio so the whole journey reads in order without narration.
- **Wed 06-09.** Edit: cut what does not earn its place, log every cut, then check all four AOs in writing.
- **Thu 06-10.** Supervised final quality control, page by page against the checklist. Fixes only.
- **Fri 06-11.** A4: the mid-point portfolio is due and submitted in session, marked against AO1–AO4. No edits after. Homework: write the semester reflection.

## Assessment

You sit **A4** (mid-point portfolio) on **Fri 2027-06-11**, marked against AO1–AO4 — see the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s2-assessments|full register]]. It is the whole investigation so far, sequenced and edited: recording, exploration, artist pages, development, one resolved piece, and the research paper draft. The sequence must read the whole journey in order without narration, and there are no edits after submission.

In S17 (Mon 2027-06-13 → 19) you meet the End-of-Term category (20%) through the **Final**: a teacher-marked assessment of your portfolio-so-far against the Cambridge criteria. There is no exam paper, no invigilation, and no new task. Your mid-point portfolio is returned to you one to one. The Final's format will be confirmed in class.

In S18 (Mon 2027-06-20 → 26) the summer production briefing sets teacher targets piece by piece, the largest uninterrupted C3 window before the investigation resumes in [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-3-plan|Semester 3]] (autumn 2027, C3 part 2).

These spring and summer dates are provisional and may shift when the school publishes the calendar for that period.

## Review

Check you can:

- Photograph your work properly, remembering the photograph is the version the examiner sees.
- Sequence the mid-point portfolio so the whole journey reads in order without narration.
- Edit by cutting what does not earn its place, and log every cut.
- Check all four Assessment Objectives (AO1–AO4): recording, exploration, development, and presentation.
- Pass supervised quality control (QC) and submit at A4, with nothing edited after.

## Practice

Use these across submission week. The craft is the same one C1 taught, now with a paper inside it.

**1.** Recite the mid-point portfolio's sequence.

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Theme, recording, exploration, artists, development, the resolved piece, the paper draft. The whole journey in order, readable by a stranger without narration. The paper draft rides inside the portfolio, not beside it.

</details>

**2.** What does Thursday's supervised QC check, and what is its rule?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Page by page against the checklist: images clean, bibliography integrated, paper draft in place, sequence reading. Fixes only, nothing new starts. The photograph passes QC only if it is the version you want the examiner to see, because it is the version the examiner sees.

</details>

**3.** Wednesday cuts and logs. Why both?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Cutting protects the strong work from the weak page beside it. Logging records the editorial judgment: what went, and why. The cut log is evidence that the editing was decisions, not accidents.

</details>

**4.** What is the Final, the week after submission?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

The End-of-Term category is met by a teacher-marked assessment of your portfolio-so-far against the Cambridge criteria. No exam paper, no invigilation, no new task. The mid-point portfolio comes back one to one, and the format is confirmed in class.

</details>

**5.** The summer is the largest uninterrupted C3 window before Semester 3. Arrive at the production briefing already knowing what you would set yourself: which pieces, what recording, which paper sections. Targets you helped set are targets you keep.
