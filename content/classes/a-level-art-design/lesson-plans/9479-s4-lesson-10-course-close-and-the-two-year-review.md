## Overview

This is the last session of the two-year course. Nothing new is taught. You look back over the whole A-Level: C1, C3 and C2, the pipeline run three times. You take back everything the board does not hold, which is the record you keep. You then hear a briefing on the A2 season ahead. These dates are projected and may shift when the school publishes the 2027-28 spring calendar.

## The ideas

### The eight-stage pipeline, run three times

The course is the **eight-stage pipeline run three times**: C1 (portfolio), C3 (personal investigation), and C2 (externally set assignment). See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-course-map|course map]]. The review reads the whole A-Level back to you: how the pipeline changed from C1 to C3 to C2, and what you can now do at pace that you could not do in September of Grade 11.

### The Final

The End-of-Term was **the Final**, not a sit-down exam. It met the 20% End-of-Term category by teacher assessment against the Cambridge criteria.

## Day by day

- **May Day week (Mon 2028-05-01 → Fri 2028-05-05).** No teaching. Reflection homework only.
- **Week of Mon 2028-05-08.** The two-year review, the portfolio return (everything the board does not hold comes back to you), and the A2-season briefing: results, the A2 timeline, and where this portfolio carries forward. No homework; the course is complete.

## Assessment

No attainment is graded this week. The 20% End-of-Term category was met by **the Final**, teacher-marked against the Cambridge criteria. The grade rules for the leaving cohort will be confirmed in class.

## Review

Check you can:

- Describe the whole A-Level as the **eight-stage pipeline run three times** across C1, C3, and C2.
- Explain how the pipeline changed from C1 to C3 to C2, and what you can now do at pace.
- Explain that the End-of-Term was met by **the Final**, teacher-marked against the Cambridge criteria.
- Say what the A2 season ahead asks of you.
