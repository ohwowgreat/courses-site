## Overview

This week you decide which of your two resolved compositions becomes the mid-point piece, and why. You critique both against your intention, act on what you hear, run targeted tests on what is still unresolved, and choose the final direction. You produce **CS8**, the targeted tests plus your critique response, due Fri 2027-05-21 in your sketchbook. The written rejection of the composition you drop is part of this work. It feeds A3, the development milestone and paper draft 1, which you sit Fri 2027-05-28.

## The ideas

### Critique against the intention

A **structured critique** judges each composition against your stated intention, not against the effort you put in. The question is whether each piece does what the investigation set out to do. Hours spent do not count. Fit to intention does.

A critique only counts when you act on it. A critique you nod at and ignore is worthless. The mark is in the **critique response**: you change something in the work because of what the critique told you.

### Targeted tests

**Targeted tests** are surgical tests on named problems. You test only what is still unresolved, the specific passages the critique flagged. This is not a fresh round of exploration. You read the test results back into your decision and note what they settle.

### Choosing is development

Choosing the final direction is development in its own right. You pick which composition carries into the mid-point piece, and you **write the rejection**: why not the other one. A choice you can justify earns AO3. A choice by default does not. A reasoned "not this one" earns AO3 as much as the piece you keep.

## Day by day

- **Mon 05-17.** Set both compositions against your stated intention and critique each on whether it does what the investigation set out to do.
- **Tue 05-18.** Act on the critique: change something in the work because of what you heard.
- **Wed 05-19.** Run targeted tests on the passages the critique flagged.
- **Thu 05-20.** Finish the tests and read the results into your decision.
- **Fri 05-21.** Choose which composition carries into the mid-point piece and write out the rejection. Submit **CS8**, the targeted tests and your critique response. Homework (≤30 min): write evaluation notes toward A3 and consolidate the paper draft, ahead of Lesson 13.

## Assessment

**CS8** (targeted tests + critique response) is due **Fri 2027-05-21**, a sketchbook task marked on whether you acted on the critique and tested the right things. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s2-assessments|full register]]. The written rejection matters: a choice you can justify is AO3; a choice by default is not. ## Review

Check you can:

- Critique both compositions against your intention rather than your effort.
- Act on a critique by changing the work in response to it.
- Run targeted tests on only what is still unresolved.
- Choose the final direction and write out the rejection as AO3 development.
