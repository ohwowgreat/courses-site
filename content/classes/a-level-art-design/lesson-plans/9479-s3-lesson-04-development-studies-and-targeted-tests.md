## Overview

This week you push your candidate compositions until they decide things. You run **targeted tests** on what is still unresolved, then change the work on the evidence those tests give you. On Friday you take part in a structured critique that reads the candidates against your intention. Your deliverable is a set of targeted tests in the sketchbook, graded as **CS4** on Fri 2027-09-24. What survives this week is refined toward the evaluation and the Stage 6 page in [[classes/a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/9479-s3-lesson-05-evaluation-and-the-national-day-break|L05]].

## The ideas

### Development studies

A **development study** is a study that answers a real question the composition poses. Each one decides something. It is not extra work added to fill the page.

### Targeted tests

A **targeted test** runs on only what is unresolved: a color relationship, a passage that is not reading clearly, a material that may not hold. A targeted test changes the composition **because of evidence**. It changes the work in response to the test result, not to a preference. A test that changes nothing was decoration.

On the Stage 6 page, your development must read as **tested, rejected, decided**. It should not look like a gallery of effort.

### Judging against the intention

The critique judges the candidates **against the intention, not the effort**. This is the standard the whole investigation is marked by. You read the work against what you set out to do, not against how much work went into it.

## Day by day

- **Mon 09-20.** Studio: development studies. Each study answers a question the composition poses.
- **Tue 09-21 and Wed 09-22.** Targeted tests, a two-day run. Test only what is unresolved: a color relationship, a passage that is not reading, a material that may not hold.
- **Thu 09-23.** Studio: act on the tests. Change the work because of the test result, not a preference.
- **Fri 09-24.** Structured critique. Read the candidates against the intention, not the effort. CS4 marks the targeted tests. Homework (≤30 min): write up one test, what it asked, what it showed, what you changed.

## Review

Check you can:

- Make a **development study** that answers a real question the composition poses.
- Run a **targeted test** on only what is unresolved.
- Change your composition because of evidence, not decoration.
- Show development that reads as **tested, rejected, decided** on the Stage 6 page.
- Judge the candidates against the intention, not the effort.
