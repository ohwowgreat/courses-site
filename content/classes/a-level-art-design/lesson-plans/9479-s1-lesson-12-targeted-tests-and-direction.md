## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Run tests only on what the critique left unresolved, with no decoration
2. Choose the final direction and write down what was rejected and why
3. Assemble the Stage 6 page so the development reads as a sequence of decisions

## Goal

No decoration. The tests exist only to answer what the critique left unresolved. You choose the direction and write down the rejection, and the development trail becomes readable as a sequence of decisions on the Stage 6 page. This is where top-band AO3 is won or lost.

## How it runs

- **Wed 11-25 → Thu 11-26** — two-day tests run: list the unresolved questions from critique, plan one test per question. Day 2 opens with the criteria read and the A3 date on the board (Thu 2026-12-10), then complete the tests, each labeled with the question it answered.
- **Fri 11-27** — lay out the tests and both compositions; choose the final direction; write the rejection in full sentences; **CS9 collected** (tests plus critique response).
- **Mon 11-30** — write the evaluation: what the development achieved against the intention, where it fell short, and what the final piece must therefore do. A frame is provided, then removed; peer read for honesty.
- **Tue 12-01** — build the Stage 6 page as a decision trail: studies, compositions, critique, tests, choice; photograph and file. Brief for the Juniors Days gap (Wed–Fri).

## Assessment

**CS9** (targeted tests + critique response, written annotation) is collected Fri 2026-11-27 — direct practice for A3 — see the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s1-assessments|full register]]. Every test answers a named unresolved question.
