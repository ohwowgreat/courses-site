## Overview

This lesson opens Unit 3 and its exploration stage. You plan and run twelve media experiments to test which media can carry your investigation of the theme. You document the twelve for **CS4**, due Fri 2027-04-02, and then select on evidence which two or three media to develop. That selection feeds the artist study and A2 later in the unit.

## The ideas

### Media exploration

**Media exploration** is the exploration stage of Unit 3: testing which media can actually carry your investigation. The point is not twelve tidy samples. It is finding the two or three media you will develop, and being able to say why.

### Experiments built to fail

Design twelve experiments across media. Each tests a real question about the theme: mark, surface, color, transparency, or scale. Build each so it *could* fail. A safe experiment that can only succeed proves nothing. The uncomfortable, less familiar media often carry the investigation furthest.

### Selecting on evidence

Select on evidence, not on taste. Keep what works, and name what you reject: a reasoned "no" is exploration too. The record matters as much as the result, so photograph and label each experiment with the question it tested and what it showed.

## Day by day

- **Mon 03-29.** Plan the media exploration: design twelve experiments across media, each testing a real question about the theme.
- **Tue 03-30.** Run the first block of experiments from your own recording. Work fast and document as you go.
- **Wed 03-31.** Push into less familiar media.
- **Thu 04-01.** Finish the twelve. Photograph and label each with the question it tested and what it showed.
- **Fri 04-02.** Select on evidence: which two or three media carry the theme, and which you reject. Name the rejections. CS4 documents the twelve. Over the Qingming mock week that follows (S7, 04-04 → 10), an independent sketchbook brief is set if the school permits.

## Review

Check you can:

- Plan and run twelve media experiments built so they could fail.
- Explain why an experiment that can only succeed tells you nothing.
- Select media on evidence, keeping what works.
- Name the media you reject and say why.
- Identify the two or three media you will develop.

## Practice

Try each question before you open its answer. Twelve experiments, one goal: the two or three media you will develop, and why.

**1.** Every experiment tests a real question about the theme. Name the five question areas.

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Mark, surface, color, transparency, scale. An experiment without a question attached is a sample, and twelve tidy samples are not exploration. Each one gets designed on Monday with its question written first.

</details>

**2.** Why must the experiments be built so they could fail?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Because a test that can only succeed tells you nothing. The experiment exists to find out whether a medium can carry the investigation, and finding out means risking a no. The uncomfortable media often carry the investigation furthest, which is exactly why Wednesday pushes into them.

</details>

**3.** What is CS4 marked on?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

The range and honesty of the testing, not tidy results. Twelve experiments across genuinely different media, documented as you go, with the failures owned. The record matters as much as the result.

</details>

**4.** State Thursday's labeling rule.

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Each experiment is photographed and labeled with the question it tested and what it showed. The label turns a stained square of paper into evidence. Without it, next week you will not remember what the stain proved.

</details>

**5.** Why do the named rejections count as exploration?

<details class="reveal"><summary>Show answer</summary>

Because a reasoned no is a decision with evidence, and the selection is only as strong as what it beat. Friday's keep-or-change names which two or three media carry the theme and which are rejected, and both lists carry reasons.

</details>
