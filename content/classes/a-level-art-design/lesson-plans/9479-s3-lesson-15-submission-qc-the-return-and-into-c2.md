## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Assemble the PDF submission to the Cambridge screen limits and pass it through QC
2. Review your returned investigation one to one and repair what QC named
3. Say what Component 2 will ask of you in the spring

## Goal

Close Component 3 and turn toward Component 2. In the two W18 sessions you assemble the PDF to the Cambridge screen limits and run submission QC, so the work is board-ready before the break. Then your returned investigation is reviewed one to one, supervised gap repair catches anything the QC named, and a C2 preview points you at the spring. The End-of-Term Final falls in the January window that follows.

## How it runs

- **W18 (≈2 sessions) — PDF assembly and submission QC.** The work is assembled to the Cambridge screen limits; every page must render; the QC is the last mark that can be earned. These are the only two spare sessions in the tail, so attendance matters — Carnival and New Year may each take one.
- **W19 — the return, 1:1.** Your complete investigation is returned and reviewed one to one; supervised gap repair for anything QC flagged; a **C2 preview** — what the Externally Set Assignment is and how the spring runs. Last teaching day ~Fri 2028-01-07.
- **W20 — the Final (EoT window, no teaching).** The 20% End-of-Term category is met by **the Final** — a teacher-marked assessment of the portfolio against the Cambridge criteria; no paper, no invigilation; date at the teacher's discretion.
- **W21 — course closes.** C2 readiness briefing; grades and comments due ~Wed 2028-01-19.

## Key content: board-ready before the break, then into C2

- ◆ **The two W18 QC sessions are the only spare time in the tail** — the submission is board-ready before the break, not after it.
- The PDF meets the Cambridge screen limits and every page renders — a submission that fails to open earns nothing.
- The **End of Term is the Final**, not a sit-down exam: this class sits no department finals, so the January window is free for marking. See the [[classes/a-level-art-design/assessments/9479-s3-assessments|register]].
- Component 3 is done; the drawing regimen and past-paper review over the vacation open Component 2 in [[classes/a-level-art-design/unit-plans/9479-semester-4-plan|Semester 4]].
