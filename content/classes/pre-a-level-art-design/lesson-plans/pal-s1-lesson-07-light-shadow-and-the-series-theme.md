## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson you can:

1. Shoot one subject in four light positions (front, side, diffused, back) and name the mood each produced
2. Control exposure on your own device, whether phone or camera
3. Write and get approved one A2 series theme that you can shoot within the fortnight

## Focus

This lesson has two halves with one join. First: light has *direction and quality*, and moving yourself (or the lamp) is a decision as expressive as framing. Second: A2 is not five nice photos — it is a **series**, one idea looked at repeatedly with intention. The join is the theme: by the end of the double you have one, in writing, approved.

## How it runs

- **Warm-up (10 min)** — in homework pairs: one thirds frame and one broken-thirds frame each; your partner guesses which was deliberate. Add vocabulary terms 20–24 (directional / ambient / soft light…) to your sketchbook glossary.
- **Teach: light (15 min)** — *Seeing Photographs* §1 on screen: **front, side, diffused, back** light, one exemplar each; hard vs soft shadows. Live demo: one object, one **continuous lamp**, walked around the four positions. Exposure control on whatever you have: phones — **tap to focus, AE/AF lock, exposure slider**; cameras — **AE lock and exposure compensation**.
- **Light circuit (20 min)** — in pairs: one subject (a classmate or an object), four frames — window side-light, backlight, diffused, hard lamp. Same subject, four moods; annotate which position made which mood.
- **Teach: the series (15 min)** — what makes photographs *a series*: one subject, idea, or rule; variation with intention; sequence. Exemplars from the shared library's Photographers collection — **Ueda's project folders, Hara's series, Atget's Versailles, Johansson's towns** — plus Cartier-Bresson's decisive moment from §4. Then the **A2 brief**: 6–8 photographs on one theme, selected from many, sequenced, titled, with a short reflection — made over the next two weeks, submitted in class W10.

- **Theme workshop (15 min)** — shortlist three candidate themes (things you can shoot at school or home this fortnight: doors, hands, red, lunch, shadows, pairs…); talk each through with a partner; **write your chosen one in your sketchbook and get it initialed** before you leave.
- **Close (10 min)** — A2 announced and posted on MB; homework: at least 15 theme frames before next Wednesday.

## Assessment

**A2** (photographic series + reflection, Wed 2026-11-04) is announced today — 14 days, two classes ahead. Marking focus: **framing, viewpoint, light, series thinking**. **SB1**, the first sketchbook spot-check, is taken during the studio blocks — it checks progress and upkeep (2–3 pages a week since W1), not a full grading.
