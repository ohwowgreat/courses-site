# Unit 4: Graphic Design & the Poster

[[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/unit-plans/pal-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 4 of 4 · ← [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/unit-plans/pal-s1-unit-3-collage|Unit 3: Collage]]

## What this unit does

Spine: **"Communicate · audience."** Three Wednesday doubles (W16, W17, W19) turn composition into communication. W18 has no class because of the winter break (Thu 2026-12-24 → Sun 2027-01-03). You start by analyzing what a poster must do (message, audience, hierarchy) and testing thumbnails and type, then make your final poster — handmade or digital — for A4, then document your work. The last session closes the semester with the End-of-Term portfolio and the A5 sketchbook submission, both handed in that day.

## Poster or printmaking

This unit runs in one of two forms across the same three Wednesdays. The default is the **Poster** route, described below. A **Printmaking** route ([[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/lesson-plans/pal-s1-u4-printmaking-variant|full variant plan]]) may run instead — this will be confirmed in class, and A4 will be announced at least one week before you sit it.

| | **Poster** | **Printmaking** |
|---|---|---|
| A4 task | Final poster, handmade or digital | Final linocut print |
| A4 marking focus | Hierarchy, typography, message, audience | Carving control, ink and registration, two-tone legibility |
| CS4 | Thumbnail set + poster analysis | Proof + annotation |

The [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pal-resource-library|Introduction Survey]] in session 1 asks you to self-rate "Print Making" and name a preferred area of study.

## Assessment

**Full brief** (objectives, task, instructions, rubric): [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a4-final-poster|A4 Poster]].

- **A4 (Attainment)** — **2026-12-23 (W17)**: final poster + reflection, handmade or digital. Announced 2026-12-16 (W16), one week before. Marking focus: **hierarchy, typography, message, audience**.
- **CS4 (Course Skills)** — **2026-12-16 (W16)**: thumbnail set + poster analysis, in the same double that announces A4. Focus: hierarchy and audience.
- **EoT (End of Term)** — 2027-01-06 (W19): semester portfolio (four finals + process + reflections). Submitted rather than sat, so the exam window does not apply.
- **A5 (Attainment)** — 2027-01-06 (W19): the **sketchbook**, submitted for its terminal attainment mark on the same day as the portfolio. Marking focus: ideas, experimentation, visual research, reflection, presentation. Full detail in the [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-assessments|register]].
