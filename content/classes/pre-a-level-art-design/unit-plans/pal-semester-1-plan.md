# Pre A Level Art & Design · Semester 1 Plan

> [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · Semester 1 (Grade 10 Art)

**Grade 10 Art, Autumn 2026-27.** You have one double session every Wednesday. There are 17 sessions, from **2026-09-02 to 2027-01-06**, plus a return session on **2027-01-20**. There are four units. Each unit closes with a final made in class. A lost Wednesday costs a whole unit stage, so try not to miss one. Dates follow the [[calendar|school calendar]]. Juniors Days are for Grade 11 only, so your Wednesday classes still run on those days. Some dates may shift when the school publishes the calendar for the semester.

## Grading

Your grade uses this scale:

A\* ≥ 90 · A ≥ 80 · B ≥ 70 · C ≥ 60 · D ≥ 50 · E ≥ 40 · U ≥ 0.

The grade has four parts: Attainment 50, Course Skills 20, Learning Behaviour 10, Final 20. See [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|BNDS Assessment Framework]].
