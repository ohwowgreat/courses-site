# Pre A Level Art & Design · Semester 1 Plan

> [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · Semester 1 (Grade 10 Art)

**Grade 10 Art, Autumn 2026-27.** You have one double session every Wednesday: 17 sessions from 2026-09-02 to 2027-01-06, plus a return session on 2027-01-20. The double runs Wed P3+P4, 9:50–11:30, in room 710. There are four units. Each unit closes on a final made in class. A lost Wednesday costs a whole unit stage, so attendance matters. Dates follow the [[calendar|school calendar]] and may shift if the school changes it.
