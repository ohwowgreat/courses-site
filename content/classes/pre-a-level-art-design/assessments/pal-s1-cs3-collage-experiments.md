## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Put two images together so the pair says something new.
2. Change the size of an image to change its meaning.
3. Build layers so the order matters.
4. Place words inside an image.
5. Write what an experiment tried, even when it failed.

## Description

I check your sketchbook for the collage experiments from this unit, plus anything you tried at home.

The four class experiments:

1. **Juxtaposition.** Two images side by side that argue with each other.
2. **Scale.** The same image large and small, and what changes.
3. **Layering.** Three or more pieces, one on top of another.
4. **Text and image.** Words placed so they change how you read the picture.

Under each one, write one line: what you tried, and what happened.

## Guidelines

- Experiments belong in the sketchbook, dated.
- Add your home experiments. They count.
- Annotate every experiment with one line. What did you try. What happened.
- Keep the failures. A failed experiment with an honest line under it scores. A failed experiment with nothing under it does not.
- Five different experiments that each say something beat ten that say nothing.
- Bring the book on Wednesday 25 November.

## Rubric

| | **Range** | **Annotation** | **Risk** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | All four types, plus work of your own | Every experiment says what it tried and what happened | You tried things that could fail, and some did |
| **Solid** | All four types present | Most annotated | Safe choices, done well |
| **Not yet** | One or two types repeated | No writing | Every experiment looks like the class demonstration |
