## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Draw a line that follows what you see, without shading.
2. Make a value scale and use it in a drawing.
3. Record three different textures with pencil.
4. Draw the space around an object instead of the object.

## Description

I check your sketchbook for four exercises from the drawing unit. You made all four in class, so this is not new work. I am checking that each one is finished, dated, and in the book.

The four exercises:

1. **Contour drawing.** Line only. No shading.
2. **Value scale and one tonal study.** Light to dark in even steps.
3. **Texture grid.** Three or more surfaces, drawn with pencil marks.
4. **Negative space study.** The shape of the gaps, not the object.

## Guidelines

- Bring the sketchbook on Wednesday 23 September.
- Write the date on every page.
- Finish any exercise you did not finish in class before that day.
- Do not redraw an exercise to make it neat. A rough study that looked hard at the object scores higher than a clean one that did not.
- If you are absent, tell me before the class, not after.

## Rubric

Three things are marked. Each one is marked on its own.

| | **Complete** | **Observation** | **Tone and texture** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | All four exercises, finished and dated | You drew what was in front of you, including the hard parts | Full range from light to dark. Textures look different from each other |
| **Solid** | All four present, one unfinished | Mostly drawn from looking | Good range. Some textures repeat the same mark |
| **Not yet** | Exercises missing | Drawn from memory or copied | One narrow band of grey |

Keep these four pages. They are the same four skills that [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a1-observational-drawing|A1]] asks for one week later.
