# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 CS4: Poster Thumbnails

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Read someone else's poster and say how it works.
2. Plan four different designs before choosing one.
3. Order what the eye sees first, second and third.
4. Choose a typeface for a reason.

## Description

Two things, handed in together.

**The poster analysis.** One existing poster, annotated against four questions: Who is it for? What does it want you to do? What do you see first? Where does your eye go next?

**Four thumbnails.** A thumbnail is a small, quick drawing of a whole design. Four different layouts for your own poster, not four versions of one layout. Add your type studies. Then circle the one you will build, and write one sentence saying why.

The thumbnail you circle is the one you make for [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a4-final-poster|A4]] next week. Choose carefully.

## Guidelines

- Four thumbnails, genuinely different from each other.
- Each thumbnail shows where the words go and where the image goes.
- Type studies: try at least three typefaces and see which suits your message.
- Circle one thumbnail and give one reason.
- Test your circled thumbnail: hold it at arm's length. Can you still tell what it is about?
- Bring everything on Wednesday 16 December.

## Rubric

| | **Analysis** | **Range of ideas** | **Hierarchy** | **Choice** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | Answers all four questions with evidence from the poster | Four genuinely different layouts | You can say what the eye hits first, second, third | The reason names the audience or the message |
| **Solid** | Answers the questions | Four layouts, two of them similar | A clear first read | A reason given |
| **Not yet** | Describes the poster instead of analysing it | One idea drawn four times | Everything the same size | Circled with no reason |

A4 sits one week after this. This task is where the thinking happens, so the poster session is only making.
