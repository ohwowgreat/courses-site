# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 SB1: Sketchbook Check 1

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Keep a sketchbook without being reminded.
2. Date your work so the order is clear.
3. Show two to three pages of work for every week.

## Description

I walk around the room and look at your sketchbook for two minutes. I am looking for **progress and upkeep**, nothing else.

This is not a grade for how good the drawings are. It is a grade for whether the book is alive. Two to three pages a week since Week 1, dated. That is the whole standard.

You have known both check dates since Week 1. The second one is Wednesday 2 December.

## Guidelines

- Bring the book on Wednesday 21 October.
- Two to three pages a week, from Week 1 onward.
- Date every page.
- Keep the failures in. A book with no failed experiments shows less, not more.
- Do not fill eight pages the night before. Work made in one night looks like work made in one night.
- Class exercises count. Home experiments count. Notes and small sketches count.

## Rubric

| | **Upkeep** | **Dating** | **Range** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | Two to three pages every week, no gaps | Every page dated | Class work, home work, experiments, notes |
| **Solid** | Most weeks covered, one or two thin | Most pages dated | Mostly class work |
| **Not yet** | Long empty stretches | Undated, order unclear | Only work I asked for in class |

The book earns marks three times this semester: here, again at SB2, and as [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a5-sketchbook|A5]] in January. Keeping it weekly is the cheapest grade in the course.
