# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 Course Skills and Homework

[[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-assessments|S1 Assessment Register]] · every Course Skills item, in plain words

The five attainments have their own pages. The smaller checks are collected here, so you always know what is being looked at, when, and what counts. None of them is a test you can fail by surprise. Each one checks work you have already been doing in class.

## How these count

**Course Skills is 20% of your grade.** It is made of four CS checks, one holiday photo task, and two sketchbook spot-checks. Notice is at least **72 hours**, and most dates are known from the start of the unit. The marking is the same everywhere: **completeness and honest looking, not polish.** A rough study that looked hard at its subject scores. A neat one that did not, does not.

## The four CS checks

### CS1 · The exercise set

**Wed 2026-09-23.** Your sketchbook is checked for the four exercise pieces from the drawing unit: the contour work, the value scale and tonal study, the texture grid, and the negative-space study. All four should already be in the book, because they were made in class. It is announced together with A1 on 09-16.

### CS2 · Composition set and the contact-sheet edit

**Wed 2026-10-28.** Two things together: your composition practice from the photography unit (the viewpoint circuit and the thirds homework), and your **contact sheet** with 12 keepers circled and 6 to 8 series candidates marked. It is marked on **framing and selection**: honest reasons on your keeps and your cuts count more than pretty frames. "Cut: same as frame 4 but worse light" is exactly the kind of sentence that scores.

### CS3 · The collage experiment set

**Wed 2026-11-25.** The experiment set from the collage unit: the juxtaposition study, the scale study, the layering study, the text-and-image study, plus your home experiments. It is marked on **range and honest annotation**: five different experiments that each say what they tried beat ten that say nothing.

### CS4 · Thumbnails and the poster analysis

**Wed 2026-12-16.** From the poster unit: one annotated poster analysis against the four questions, and your four thumbnails with type studies, one circled with a reason. It is marked on **hierarchy and audience**. You sit it one week before A4, and it is the rehearsal for it.

## The holiday task

### HW1 · The break photo task

**Set Wed 2026-09-30, due Wed 2026-10-14.** Over the National Day break: **at least twenty photos of one subject**, in different viewpoints and different light, with no editing. One subject, truly: the variation is the assignment. It is marked for looking, not luck. The photography unit's first session runs on your twenty, so the number matters more than any single frame.

## The sketchbook checks

### SB1 and SB2 · Spot-checks

**Wed 2026-10-21 and Wed 2026-12-02.** The sketchbook is checked twice during the semester, table by table, for **progress and upkeep**: two to three pages a week since the start, dated. It is a two-minute look, not a full grading. Both dates are known from week one, so the only way to be caught out is to have stopped keeping the book. SB2 is the last look before the whole book is submitted as A5 on Wed 2027-01-06.

## The weekly posting

The **Learning Behaviour** 10% is the weekly rhythm: **a photo of the week's sketchbook pages posted on MB after every session.** That is seventeen postings across the semester, one per teaching week. It is the smallest task in the course and the easiest marks to keep or lose, because it only measures whether the habit is alive.

## One book, three kinds of marks

The sketchbook earns marks three ways, and it is worth seeing them together: the weekly MB posting (Learning Behaviour), the two spot-checks above (Course Skills), and the final submission as [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a5-sketchbook|A5]] (Attainment). Keep the book alive two to three pages a week and all three take care of themselves.
