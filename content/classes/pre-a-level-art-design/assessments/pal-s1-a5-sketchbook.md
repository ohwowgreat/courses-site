## Objectives

The sketchbook is the only assessment in this course that you cannot produce in a session. It asks one question: is there evidence, across a whole semester, of somebody thinking in a book?

It asks whether you can show:

1. Ideas that developed rather than arrived.
2. Experiments, including the ones that failed.
3. Visual research: things you looked at, recorded and used.
4. Reflection written as you went, not summarized at the end.
5. A book somebody else can navigate.

## The task

Hand in the sketchbook. That is the whole task.

What should be in it, built up since Week 1:

- Weekly work from every session, dated.
- Your tonal scales, texture studies and negative-space exercises from U1.
- Contact sheets and **annotated rejects** from U2.
- Collage drafts, dry-fit photographs and scraps from U3.
- Thumbnails and type studies from U4.
- The reflections written after each attainment.
- Whatever you did over the National Day and winter breaks, dated.

You have already seen this standard twice, at the SB1 and SB2 spot-checks. A5 is the same book, judged completely rather than sampled.

## Instructions

1. Bring the book on Wed 2027-01-06.
2. Before you hand it in, **number your pages** if they are not numbered, and make sure everything is dated.
3. Do not tidy it by removing failures. A sketchbook with no failed experiments in it shows less, not more.
4. Do not add three weeks of work the night before. Dated work made on the day is the point, and the difference is visible.
5. Hand it in at the close, with the portfolio.

**If you are absent.** Tell your teacher **at least 24 hours before**. Without advance contact the mark may be 0, even for genuine illness (AL Student Guide p.8). This is the last teaching session of the semester, so there is no later class to hand it to.

## Rubric

Five criteria, **weighted**:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Ideas and creativity, idea development | 25% |
| Experimentation | 25% |
| Visual research | 20% |
| Critical reflection | 15% |
| Presentation | 15% |

| | **Ideas and creativity** (25%) | **Experimentation** (25%) | **Visual research** (20%) | **Reflection** (15%) | **Presentation** (15%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perceptive** | Ideas visibly develop across pages: a thought is followed, tested, changed. Personal direction is emerging | Wide, genuine experimenting, including failures kept and annotated. The experiments feed the finals | Sustained looking, recorded and then used. Sources are noted and something is taken from each | Written as you went, naming decisions and what changed because of them | Navigable, dated, complete. The order tells the story of the semester |
| **Confident** | Ideas develop, with some jumps unexplained | Regular experimenting across media, mostly connected to the finals | Consistent visual research, mostly used | Regular reflection with clear reasons | Well kept, dated, easy to follow |
| **Competent** | Ideas are present but arrive rather than develop | Some experimenting, in the media the unit required | Visual research is collected and only sometimes used | Reflection after the big pieces, thinner in between | Complete, with some undated or unordered stretches |
| **Adequate** | Little development: mostly finished attempts side by side | Experimenting is thin, or confined to one medium | Copied or collected images with nothing taken from them | Occasional writing that describes rather than reflects | Gaps, undated pages, difficult to follow |
| **Limited** | No development visible | Almost no experimenting | Little visual research | Almost no writing | Substantially incomplete |

Under the [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|BNDS framework]] this becomes a curved grade, not a raw score, with the more generous G10 curve.

## How the sketchbook is graded across the semester

The book earns marks **three different ways**. This is worth understanding, because it means upkeep pays three times:

| When | What | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Every week, after every session | The MB posting, 17 across the semester | Learning Behaviour, 10% |
| Wed 2026-10-21 and Wed 2026-12-02 | The **SB1** and **SB2** spot-checks: progress and upkeep | Course Skills, 20% |
| Wed 2027-01-06 | **A5**, the whole book against the criteria above | Attainment, 50% |

The spot-checks are **not** mini-versions of A5. They look at progress and upkeep only. A5 is the only time the full criteria are applied.

## After the sit

- Books come back at the **return session, Wed 2027-01-20**, with grades, comments and one-to-one feedback.
- A5 and the EoT portfolio together produce the semester's final grades.
- Keep the book. Semester 2 continues in it, and in G11 the same habit becomes the AO1 recording layer of a real Cambridge portfolio.

## How to prepare

There is no preparing for A5 in the last week. You prepare it by keeping it every week, which is the reason the weekly MB posting exists.

The one useful thing to do before submission: **read your own book front to back.** Then number and date anything that is missing either. You will find the gaps faster than your teacher will, and that is a page of reflection in itself.
