# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 CS2: Composition and Editing

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Frame a photograph on purpose instead of pointing and shooting.
2. Read a contact sheet and find your strongest frames.
3. Say why you keep a photograph and why you cut one.
4. Narrow many photographs down to a few.

## Description

Two things, handed in together.

**The composition practice.** Your viewpoint circuit and your rule-of-thirds work from class.

**The contact sheet.** A contact sheet is one page of small versions of all your photographs. On it, circle your **12 keepers**. Then mark **6 to 8** of those as candidates for your series.

Next to each keeper and each cut, write one short reason. The reason is what I mark. "Cut: same as frame 4 but worse light" is a good sentence. "Nice" is not a reason.

## Guidelines

- Print the contact sheet, or lay it out on screen. Either is fine.
- Circle 12 keepers.
- Mark 6 to 8 series candidates inside those 12.
- Write one reason for each keeper and each cut. One line each.
- Be honest. Cutting a photograph you like, and saying why, scores higher than keeping everything.
- Bring it on Wednesday 28 October.

## Rubric

| | **Framing** | **Selection** | **Reasons** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | Frames are composed. What is left out was left out on purpose | 12 keepers and 6 to 8 candidates, and the choices hold up | Every reason names something in the picture |
| **Solid** | Mostly composed, some frames centred by habit | The right number, one or two questionable | Most reasons are specific |
| **Not yet** | The subject sits in the middle of every frame | No selection made, or everything kept | Reasons are opinions, not observations |
