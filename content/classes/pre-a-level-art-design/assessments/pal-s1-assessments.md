# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 Assessments

[[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/unit-plans/pal-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]]

This is the full assessment register for Grade 10 Art, Semester 1 (2026-27). Work is graded under the BNDS A-Level Handbook 25-26 categories. See [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|BNDS Assessment Framework]] for the framework. Weights: **Attainment 50% · Course Skills 20% · Learning Behaviour 10% · End of Term Exam 20%**.

Each attainment has its own page with objectives, the task, instructions, and a rubric. There is also the [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-eot-semester-portfolio|EoT portfolio]] page. A3 is marked on its announced focus. A5 carries one attainment grade with an internal 100-mark breakdown.

### A5 — the sketchbook

The fifth attainment is the sketchbook itself. You submit it for assessment on the last teaching session, **Wed 2027-01-06 (W19), alongside the End-of-Term portfolio.** It carries the whole semester's work, so you know about it from the first week. It is assessed on ideas and creativity, experimentation, visual research, critical reflection, and presentation.

**Sketchbook spot-checks (SB1, SB2):** your sketchbook is checked twice during the semester for a Course Skills mark, on **Wed 2026-10-21** and **Wed 2026-12-02**. Both dates are known in advance. The focus is progress and upkeep, not a full grade against the A5 rubric.

## Learning Behaviour (10%)

Post your sketchbook on MB after every session. That is **17 postings** across the semester; there is no posting in W18 because of the winter break. This carries the one-grade-per-week rule.

The sketchbook earns marks three ways: the weekly posting here (Learning Behaviour), two spot-checks during the semester (Course Skills: **SB1** 2026-10-21, **SB2** 2026-12-02), and the final submission (Attainment **A5**, due 2027-01-06).

## End of Term (20%)

**Semester portfolio due Wed 2027-01-06 (W19):** the four finals, process work, and reflections. It is reviewed during the exam window (2027-01-11 → 01-18). A studio course has no defined EoT sitting, so this portfolio is the End-of-Term form.
