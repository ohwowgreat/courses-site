Each attainment has its own page with objectives, the task, instructions and a rubric. See also the [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-eot-semester-portfolio|EoT portfolio]].

### A5 — the sketchbook

The fifth attainment is the sketchbook itself. You submit it for assessment on the last teaching session, **Wed 2027-01-06 (W19)**, alongside the End-of-Term portfolio. It holds the whole semester's work, so you know about it from the first week. It is assessed on ideas and creativity, experimentation, visual research, critical reflection, and presentation.

**Sketchbook spot-checks (SB1, SB2):** your sketchbook is checked twice during the semester for a Course Skills mark, on **Wed 2026-10-21** and **Wed 2026-12-02**. Both dates are known in advance. The focus is progress and upkeep, not a full grade against the A5 rubric.

## Learning Behaviour (10%)

Post your sketchbook on MB after every session: **17 postings** across the semester. There is no posting in W18 because of the winter break. This carries the one-grade-per-week rule.

The sketchbook earns marks three ways: the weekly posting here (Learning Behaviour), two spot-checks during the semester (Course Skills: **SB1** 2026-10-21, **SB2** 2026-12-02), and the final submission (Attainment **A5**, due 2027-01-06).

## End of Term (20%)

**Semester portfolio due Wed 2027-01-06 (W19):** the four finals, process work and reflections. It is reviewed during the exam window (2027-01-11 → 01-18). This studio course has no separate EoT sitting; the portfolio review is the End of Term assessment.
