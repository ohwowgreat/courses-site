# Pre A Level Art & Design · S1 SB2: Sketchbook Check 2

[[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|Pre A Level Art & Design]] · [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-assessments|S1 Assessment Register]] · Course Skills

| | |
|---|---|

| Category | Course Skills, 20% of your grade |
| Bring | Your sketchbook |
| How long | Two minutes at your table |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Keep the book running through a whole semester, not only at the start.
2. Show the process behind your finished work.
3. Find your own gaps before someone else does.

## Description

The second and last sketchbook check before the book is submitted as [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/assessments/pal-s1-a5-sketchbook|A5]] in January.

Same standard as SB1: two to three pages a week, dated, progress and upkeep. What is different now is that you have process material worth keeping. Your contact sheet and your cut photographs from the photography unit belong in the book. So do your collage drafts and your leftover scraps.

This is the last time I look at the book before it is graded fully. Use it.

## Guidelines

- Bring the book on Wednesday 2 December.
- Two to three pages a week since SB1.
- Date every page.
- Paste in your contact sheet and your rejected photographs.
- Paste in collage drafts, dry-fit photos and scraps.
- Ask me on the day about any gap you already know about.

## Rubric

| | **Upkeep** | **Process evidence** | **Dating** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | Two to three pages every week since SB1 | Contact sheet, rejects, drafts and scraps all in the book | Every page dated |
| **Solid** | Most weeks covered | Some process material | Most pages dated |
| **Not yet** | The book stopped after SB1 | Only finished work, no process | Undated |

After this, the next time the book is opened for a grade it is worth 50%. Nothing you keep now is wasted.
