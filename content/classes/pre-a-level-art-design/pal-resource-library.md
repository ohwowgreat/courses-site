# Pre A Level Art & Design · Resource Library

This page lists the teaching material for the course. See [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/unit-plans/pal-semester-1-plan|the semester plan]] for what is taught when.

## Image sets

**Visual Elements sample work** holds 17 exemplars for visual-elements analysis, weighted toward photography:

- **Photography:** Walker Evans (*Alabama Cotton Tenant Farmer Wife*, 1936), August Sander (*Artists' Party in Cologne*, c.1930), Carleton Watkins (*Arbutus Menziesii*), Emmet Gowin (*Edith, Chincoteague*, 1967), Frederick H. Evans (*Lincoln Cathedral*, 1898), Eugène Atget (*Versailles* ×2, 1901–02), Gerry Johansson (×2), Albert Elm
- **Painting / works on paper:** Ad Reinhardt (*Number 107*), Adolph Gottlieb (*Blast I*, *Descending Arrow*), Arnulf Rainer (*Untitled*), Fernand Léger (*The Mirror*), Jacob Lawrence (*They were very poor*)

The set leans photographic, so it also works as a U2 Photography reference. Atget, Watkins and Evans are series-and-structure exemplars, not just visual-elements ones.

## Printmaking practicals

These resources support the printmaking part of U4.

| Item | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Linocut sequence | A three-class linocut sequence: (1) intro to relief printing, safety, design transfer via tracing paper, begin carving; (2) refine carving, ink with a brayer, test prints, first prints on good paper; (3) final prints, multi-color and layering experiments, reflection and gallery walk. Includes a materials list, formative and summative assessment, a 100% rubric, and two extensions (a 3–4 print series; artist research on Dürer, Hiroshige or a contemporary printmaker). |
| A5 drawing guide | Your task: three A5 drawings that will work as linocuts. Rules: A5 with a 1 cm border, one clear subject readable "in 2 seconds from far away," no tiny background detail. The key constraint is **"Linocut = no middle values"**: carved areas go white, so suggest "grey" with lines, cross-hatching, dots or pattern, never soft pencil shading. Ends with a five-point checklist. |
| Carving instructions | Five illustrated steps: design on paper, transfer to lino (carbon paper, or lay the lino on the drawing and rub the back to lift graphite), cut away the negative space, ink via an inking plate and roller, print with a baren, spoon or hand using circular pressure. Note the **mirror-image rule**: your print comes out reversed, which matters if the design contains text. And the **irreversibility rule**: "you can only carve more material away, but you can't put it back." |
| Color mixing guide | Sheet 1 is an RYB subtractive color-mixing poster: primary/secondary/tertiary star, equal-amount vs proportional mixes with ratios (2 red : 1 blue = magenta, and so on), tints/tones/shades, and a wavelength spectrum. Sheet 2 is a paint-recipe chart, 22 mixes on a 10-part formula (e.g. bright orange = 8 CY + 2 CR). Sheet 1 supports the multi-color printing session and works as general color theory. |
