**Concepts:** [[concepts/representation|Representation]] · [[concepts/production-craft|Production Craft]] · [[concepts/process-evidence-and-reflection|Process Evidence and Reflection]]


This unit teaches **Representation** as something that is constructed, then turns it on your own work. [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a2-blog-mid-point|A2]] opens the unit. You screen your rough cut under audience conditions and audit it for the stereotypes you did not notice, with production time left to fix them. You test gaze theory across decades instead of reciting it. You draft the CCR, review it against the real C1 criteria, and redraft it to reflect rather than describe.

*Numbering note: "U4" here is the S1 calendar band. In the coursebook's separate "Units 1–8" numbering, Representation is Unit 3.*

## Key content

- Theorists: Hall (encoding/decoding: dominant, negotiated, oppositional), van Zoonen (gender as performance), Mulvey (the three looks), bell hooks (the oppositional gaze).
- Analytical moves: construction vs reflection; who a stereotype works for; absence and selective construction; readings the text did not invite.
- C1 milestones: rough cut screened as an audience sees it; stereotype audit with a fix list ([[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-course-skills-and-homework|LB3]] evidence); complete CCR redraft posted. Cut every sentence that describes instead of reflects.
- ESL scaffold: a theorist sentence-starters card for CS10, on every desk.
