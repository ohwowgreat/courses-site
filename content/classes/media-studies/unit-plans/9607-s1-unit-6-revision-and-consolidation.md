## What this unit does

This unit audits, drills and sits the semester's knowledge. A4 feedback becomes your targets; a coverage matrix crosses every case study against the four Key Concepts to find the gaps HW2 fills over the break; exam rehearsal covers format, timing, and the mark scheme's AO economy; then you sit the full two-hour Component 2 in the school exam window and get it back as question-level AO data and a one-page Semester 2 target sheet.

*Numbering note: "U6" here is the Semester 1 calendar band (revision — it has no coursebook counterpart). L16's Semester 2 preview "U6 to U8" refers to the coursebook's separate "Units 1–8" numbering.*

## Key content

- Coverage matrix: case studies × the four Key Concepts; your weakest cell names your HW2 target.
- Section B craft: arguments rehearsed aloud (claim, evidence, counter, response) before writing; minute budgets per section; peer marking so you read the scheme as a marker once.
- Closing data loop: question-level AO breakdowns; a one-page target sheet (gap, evidence, action, review date); grades and comments due Wed 01-20.
