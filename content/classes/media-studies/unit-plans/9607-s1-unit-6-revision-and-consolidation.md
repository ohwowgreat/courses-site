You audit, drill, and sit the semester's knowledge. [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a4-section-a-authentic-format|A4]] feedback becomes your targets. The **coverage matrix** crosses every case study against the four Key Concepts to find the gaps that [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-course-skills-and-homework|HW2]] fills over the break. Exam rehearsal makes the paper routine: you practice the format, the timing, and the mark scheme's AO economy. Then you sit the full two-hour Component 2 in the school exam window. It comes back as question-level AO data and one-page Semester 2 target sheets.

*Numbering note: "U6" here is the Semester 1 calendar band for revision. It has no coursebook counterpart. L19's Semester 2 preview "U6 to U8" refers to the coursebook's separate "Units 1–8" numbering.*

## Key content

- **Coverage matrix:** case studies crossed against the four Key Concepts. Your weakest cell names your HW2 target.
- **Section B craft:** you rehearse arguments aloud (claim, evidence, counter, response) before writing. You keep to minute budgets per section. Peer marking means you read the scheme as a marker at least once.
- **Closing data loop:** question-level AO breakdowns, plus one-page target sheets (gap, evidence, action, review date) written to still make sense when you read them in February. Grades and comments are due Wed 01-20.
