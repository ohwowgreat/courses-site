# Media Studies · Semester 1 Plan

> [[classes/media-studies/media-studies|Media Studies]] · Semester 1 of 4 ([[classes/media-studies/unit-plans/9607-course-map|course map]]) · next: S2 C1 submission + Paper 2

**G11 (AS year), autumn 2026-27.** This semester runs 19 deliverable-based lessons across 58 teaching days plus 1 return day (59 dated days) in 21 weeks. The goal: master media language and textual analysis, launch, produce and internally complete **Component 1 by Fri 2026-11-27**, and establish C2 Section A craft. Dates follow the [[calendar|school calendar]]. Some later dates may shift if the school updates its calendar. Changes will be confirmed in class.

Your classes are **Mon P6, Wed P6+P7 (double), Thu P1, Fri P6**, in room 710. Every sit keeps its date. You sit **[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a1-timed-media-language-analysis|A1]] on Wed 10-14**, **[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a2-blog-mid-point|A2]] is due Mon 10-26**, **[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a3-c1-portfolio|A3]] is due Fri 11-27**, you sit **[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a4-section-a-authentic-format|A4]] on Wed 12-16**, and the **[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-eot-component-2-paper|EoT]]** falls in the exam window.
