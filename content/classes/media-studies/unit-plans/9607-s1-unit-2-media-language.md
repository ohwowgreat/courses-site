**Concepts:** [[concepts/media-language|Media Language]] · [[concepts/technical-elements|Technical Elements]] · [[concepts/production-craft|Production Craft]] · [[concepts/media-vocabulary|Media Vocabulary]]


This unit covers the micro end of **Media Language**. You start with a compact semiotic foundation. Then you study the four technical elements one lesson at a time, with equipment in hand. Then you bring everything together on one unseen extract and lock your Component 1 plan before the break. Genre and narrative are taught later, in [[classes/media-studies/unit-plans/9607-s1-unit-3-macro-and-textual-analysis|Unit 3]].

The unit follows the Scheme of Work's structure for the Foundation Portfolio: **research, practice, planning, production, reflection**. Every technical lesson ends with a shot, recording, or cut posted and dated on your C1 blog. These posts are not exercises about Component 1. They are Component 1, its research-and-practice stage, evidenced as you do it.

[[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a1-timed-media-language-analysis|A1]] sits Wed 10-14, inside Unit 3. See the [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-assessments|full register]].

## Key content

- **Semiotic base**: Saussure (signifier/signified), Peirce (icon/index/symbol), denotation and connotation, polysemy, anchorage and relay, mode of address, the four code families.
- **The four technical elements**: camera (shots, angles, movement, composition); sound (diegetic and non-diegetic, bridge, motif, perspective, soundtrack); mise-en-scène (production design, lighting, color design, with acting excluded); editing (continuity and non-continuity systems, transitions, duration).
- **Production craft**, taught where you need it rather than as a separate topic: focus and exposure control at L03, monitored audio and foley at L04, lighting set-ups at L05, the 180-degree rule and the cut at L06.
- **C1 milestones**: media area committed Wed 09-09; four dated practice posts across W3 to W5; design board Thu 09-24; equipment decision logged Mon 09-28; planning suite locked against the shootability checklist Wed 09-30; break production window; footage reviewed Fri 10-09.
- **Vocabulary**: the lexicon grows one element at a time toward [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-course-skills-and-homework|CS8]] rather than arriving as a single list. You meet each technical term first as something you did, then as a word.
