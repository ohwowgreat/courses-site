The contexts band closes Component 1 and turns the course toward Component 2. Your portfolio goes in on Friday of W13, with no edits after. Its feedback becomes your Semester 2 production targets. You then study audience and institutions theory: uses and gratifications, Morley, ownership, integration, synergy, convergence, and regulation. This builds toward your first authentic Section A sit ([[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a4-section-a-authentic-format|A4]], the Forecast Grade anchor) and opens the case-study document that carries A4, the End of Term Exam, and Paper 2.

## Key content

- Theorists: Blumler and Katz (uses and gratifications), Morley (the Nationwide study, differential readings).
- Institutions: ownership and funding (PSB vs commercial), integration, synergy, convergence, and regulation and its consequences.
- C1 closes: Component 1 is complete and graded, with one evidenced production target each for Semester 2.
- C2 opens: you open the case-study document on Fri 12-11 from a template, "a case study you will still be using in May". You update it live through L16 and feed it with homework entries.
