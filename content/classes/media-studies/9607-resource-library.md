# Media Studies · Resource Library

This course spans two exam specifications. Your AS exams (June 2027) follow the 2021 specification; your A Level exams (June 2028) follow the 2028 specification. Some exam formats differ between the two — see [[classes/media-studies/media-studies|syllabus straddle]] and the format notes below.

## The coursebook

Marshall, Naisbett & Stoklosa, *Cambridge International AS & A Level Media Studies Coursebook*, CUP (ISBN 978-1-009-26224-8), Cambridge-endorsed. Eight units:

| Unit | Book pp. |
|---|---|
| 1 Introduction to Media | 2–15 |
| 2 Media Language: Micro elements | 16–58 |
| 3 Media Language: Macro elements | 59–82 |
| 4 Textual Analysis | 83–104 |
| 5 Media Contexts | 105–136 |
| 6 Media Production: AS and A Level portfolios | 137–180 |
| 7 Media Debates | 181–221 |
| 8 Media Ecology | 222–252 |

- The practice extract *My Life is Mine* (§4.1) anchors Unit 4 — the exam-style prompt, the four-viewings protocol, and the worked note charts all use it.
- The book has **no glossary and no index** — terms are defined inline only. Figures and images are only in the print book.

## How the papers are marked

**Paper 2** (both Section A extract analysis and Section B contexts essay, 25 marks each): five criteria, each marked independently on a 0–5 band, best-fit:

| AO | Criterion | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 (15) | Media Concepts (language & representation) | /5 |
| AO1 | Contexts & Critical Debates (social significances) | /5 |
| AO1 | Use of Terminology | /5 |
| AO2 (10) | Analysis of how meaning is created, incl. use of theory | /5 |
| AO2 | Use of Examples | /5 |

Per-criterion band words: **5** Sophisticated / Insightful · **4** Thorough / Effective · **3** Clear / appropriate · **2** Limited · **1** Basic · **0** no creditable content. Paper 2 is **AO1 60 / AO2 40**.

**Paper 4:** Section A essays are scored on the same five criteria at 3 marks each; Section B (Media Ecology) uses generic level descriptors (Higher = Levels 4–5, Middle = Level 3, Basic = Levels 1–2). AO1 60 / AO2 40 overall.

Command words across all papers: Analyse / Explain / Evaluate / Assess / To what extent.

## Exam formats

**Paper 2 (AS, June 2027):** Section A asks you to analyse how the extract constructs meaning through camera, sound, mise-en-scène and editing — 25 marks, four screenings on a 30/45/45-minute rubric. Section B is a contexts essay, 25 marks. From 2028 Paper 2 becomes a digital exam in which the extract is available throughout and can be watched as many times as you like (watch once straight through before taking notes), with 1 hr 15 for Section A (viewing included) and 45 min for Section B.

**Paper 4 (A Level, June 2028):** Section A covers three topics in fixed order — regulation, postmodern media, power (15-mark essays); you answer two. Section B is a compulsory synoptic essay on changing media environments (30 marks). Paper 4 is a digital exam from 2028.

Case studies must be your own and less than five years old ([[concepts/case-study-method|five-year contemporary rule]]).

Points to note from the exemplars:

- Candidate A was well dated and showed progression but capped at Level 3 for carrying too much preliminary material and not enough on the set brief.
- Candidate B's stronger planning included recce reports, casting information, scripting, storyboarding and test shots for effects.
- Both used only **one digital application** in the CCR and lost a mark there — for Candidate B that was the only strand off the top band.
