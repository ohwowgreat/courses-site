# Media Studies · S1 CS4: Sound, the 30-Second Soundscape

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Fri 2026-09-18, in class |
| What | A 30-second sound design for the opening you are actually making |
| Marked on | AO1 + AO3 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Build a soundscape from your own recordings
2. Use diegetic and non-diegetic sound deliberately
3. Justify each choice by its term and its intended effect

## Description

A 30-second sound design for **the opening you are actually making**, built from your own recordings, Wednesday's foley takes, and one non-diegetic element.

Four things must be in it:

- one clearly **diegetic** sound
- one clearly **non-diegetic** sound
- one sound with a near or far **perspective**
- one that carries across an implied cut as a **sound bridge**

Upload it to the blog, dated, with a rationale that names each choice by its term and says what it should do to the listener.

Sound is the element Cambridge itself says candidates neglect. That makes this the cheapest advantage in the course.

## Guidelines

- Thirty seconds. Not three minutes.
- Use your own recordings. Library sound alone does not evidence the skill.
- Headphones on while you mix, or you will not hear what you have made.
- Name each of the four required elements in the rationale, using its term.
- Say what each should do to the listener, not just what it is.
- Post dated, the same day.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | All four required elements present and identifiable, terminology exact, and the rationale states an intended effect for each choice. |
| **Secure** | Four elements present, terminology mostly right, rationale states effects for most. |
| **Developing** | One or two elements missing or misidentified, or the rationale describes what was used without saying why. |
| **Not yet** | Not posted, or fewer than half the required elements present. |

Marked on terminology accuracy, the four required elements, and a rationale that states intended effect. Not on production polish.
