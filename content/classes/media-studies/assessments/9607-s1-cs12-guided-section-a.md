# Media Studies · S1 CS12: Guided Section A

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Wed 2026-12-09, in class, 50 minutes |
| What | A Section A response with the mark scheme visible |
| Marked on | AO1 and AO2 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Write to a mark scheme while you can still consult it
2. Band your own writing against the official criteria
3. Annotate your own margin the way an examiner would

## Description

A Section A response written **with the mark scheme visible and consultable**. You may stop mid-answer, check the band language, and annotate your own margin.

This is deliberately the opposite of an exam. Before you write under pressure, you write with the criteria open, so you learn what the bands actually ask for rather than guessing at them later.

Afterwards you band your own script against the five official Paper 2 criteria: concepts, contexts, terminology, analysis, examples.

## Guidelines

- Mark scheme on the desk, open, the whole time.
- Stop and check whenever you are unsure. That is the exercise.
- Annotate your margin as you go: which criterion is this sentence serving?
- Fifty minutes. Use them.
- Band yourself honestly at the end, criterion by criterion.
- Note the one criterion you scored lowest. That is your target for CS13.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | The response visibly answers the criteria, the margin annotation shows criteria awareness throughout, and the self-banding lands within one band of mine. |
| **Secure** | Response addresses most criteria, some annotation, self-banding within two bands. |
| **Developing** | Written without consulting the scheme despite it being available, or no self-banding attempted. |
| **Not yet** | Incomplete response, or no engagement with the criteria at all. |

The target is that your own banding lands within one band of mine. That calibration is the skill.
