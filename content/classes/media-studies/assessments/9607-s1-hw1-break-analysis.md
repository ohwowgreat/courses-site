# Media Studies · S1 HW1: Break Analysis and the Production Window

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Set | Wed 2026-09-30 |
| Due | Fri 2026-10-09 |
| What | An A1-format analysis, plus 3 to 4 hours of production |
| Marked on | AO1 + AO2, debriefed with margin notes rather than graded |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Run the viewing protocol on a text nobody taught you
2. Write in A1 format without a class around you
3. Shoot to your own locked plan and keep a diary while you do it

## Description

Two things across the National Day break.

**First, an A1-format analysis** of a 2 to 3 minute sequence **you choose yourself**. Not a text taught in class, and on a platform I can view. Choosing your own is part of the task: an extract with nothing happening in it gives you nothing to write about.

**Second, the production window**: 3 to 4 hours of shooting against your locked plan, with **diary entries as you go**, not reconstructed afterwards. Sign your equipment out before the holiday.

The analysis is debriefed with margin notes, annotated rather than graded. A1 is the graded one, five days later.

## Guidelines

- Choose a sequence with several things happening: camera, sound, design, cutting.
- Two to three minutes. Longer is not better and will not be read further.
- Give me a link I can open. Check it works from outside your account.
- A1 format: technique, terminology, meaning, representation.
- Production: 3 to 4 hours total, and write the diary as you shoot.
- Any departure from the locked plan is a diary entry, not a silent change.
- Equipment signed out before the break and returned on the Friday.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A well-chosen dense sequence, analysed in A1 format across three or four elements, and a production diary recording decisions and consequences as they happened. |
| **Secure** | Analysis in the right format on a reasonable choice, diary present but partly written afterwards. |
| **Developing** | Thin extract chosen, or analysis in the wrong register, or a diary reconstructed from memory. |
| **Not yet** | No link, no analysis, or no production undertaken. |

Annotated, not graded. What the margin notes say is what A1 will test.
