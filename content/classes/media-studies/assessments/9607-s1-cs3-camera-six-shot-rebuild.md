# Media Studies · S1 CS3: Camera, the Six-Shot Rebuild

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Thu 2026-09-17, shot in the second half of the session |
| What | Six shots to a storyboard you did not draw |
| Posted | To the C1 blog, dated, one line of intention per shot |
| Marked on | AO1 + AO3 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Shoot six planned shots to someone else's storyboard
2. Hold focus, exposure and framing while thinking about meaning
3. State an intention per shot in the syllabus's own terms

## Description

In pairs, you are given a **six-frame storyboard you did not draw**, and you shoot it: establishing shot, mid-shot on a character, a slow tilt to reveal, a close-up on an object, a low angle on that same character, and a point-of-view shot.

Shooting someone else's plan is the point. You cannot quietly change the brief to suit what is easy.

Post the footage to your C1 blog, dated, with **one line of intention per shot**. Under the Foundation Portfolio structure this post is your practice-stage evidence, not a warm-up exercise.

**No dialogue and no acting are needed.** The exercise is the camera.

## Guidelines

- Phones are fine. Charged, cleared, and brought to the session.
- All six shots. Missing one costs more than any of them being imperfect.
- Three controls, every take: hold focus, expose for the face, frame to the thirds.
- One line per shot, using the syllabus terms: name the shot, say what it should do.
- Post the same day, dated. A late post is a different task.
- Work in pairs, but each of you posts your own.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | All six shots recognisably to brief, three controls held throughout, and every intention line names a technique and an effect. |
| **Secure** | Six shots present and mostly to brief, controls held in most, intentions stated though some restate the shot. |
| **Developing** | Shots missing or substituted, controls slipping (face lost to a bright window, focus drifting), intentions vague. |
| **Not yet** | Not posted, undated, or the storyboard was not followed. |

Marked on terms used accurately, the three controls held, and intention stated per shot. **Not** on how good the footage looks. Acting is not assessed anywhere in this syllabus.
