# Media Studies · S1 CS13: Timed Section B

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Wed 2027-01-06, in class, 45 minutes |
| What | A full Section B answer under time, on your own case studies |
| Marked on | AO1 and AO2 |
| Then | Peer-marked against the official five-criteria rubric |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Write a full Section B answer in 45 minutes
2. Use your own case studies under pressure, from memory
3. Mark a peer against the official rubric and justify the band

## Description

A full Section B answer under time, on **your own case studies**, from a question chosen on the day.

Your case-study document has been building since Unit 5. This is where it earns its keep. Nothing is provided: the examples come from what you have prepared.

Afterwards it is **peer-marked against the official five-criteria rubric**, and the marker has to justify the band they give. Marking someone else against the rubric is how the rubric stops being abstract.

The End of Term exam follows in the window from Mon 01-11.

## Guidelines

- Bring your case-study document to revise from, not to write from.
- Five minutes planning. Write the plan down.
- Use specific examples: named texts, named details, named institutions.
- Forty-five minutes, in one sitting, no stopping to check.
- When you mark a peer, give a band per criterion and one sentence justifying each.
- Read your own returned script against the band you were given.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A complete, planned answer using specific case-study evidence throughout, addressing all five criteria. The peer-marking is justified criterion by criterion. |
| **Secure** | Complete answer with real examples, most criteria addressed, peer-marking mostly justified. |
| **Developing** | Answer thin on specific examples or unfinished, or peer-marking gives bands without reasons. |
| **Not yet** | No case-study evidence, or the answer is not completed in the time. |

This is the last rehearsal before the exam window. Treat it as one.
