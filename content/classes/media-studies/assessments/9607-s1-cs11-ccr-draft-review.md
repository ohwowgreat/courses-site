# Media Studies · S1 CS11: CCR Draft Review

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Thu 2026-11-19, in class |
| What | Your full CCR draft, reviewed against the real C1 criteria |
| Written the day before | In the Wednesday double |
| Marked on | AO3 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Bring a complete draft rather than an outline
2. Read your own work against the criteria it will actually be marked with
3. Leave with an action list written in criteria language

## Description

Your **full creative critical reflection draft**, completed in Wednesday's double, reviewed against the **real C1 criteria** by teacher and peers.

Complete matters. A review of half a draft reviews half a draft, and the C1 portfolio is due Fri 11-27, eight days later.

You leave with a **written action list in C1 criteria language**: not "make it better" but which criterion is underserved and what will serve it.

Drafts are triaged by severity, so the weakest get teacher time and the strongest get peer review. That is not a judgement, it is arithmetic.

## Guidelines

- Bring the whole draft. Gaps marked "to do" count as gaps.
- Have the C1 criteria in front of you, not from memory.
- Go criterion by criterion, not paragraph by paragraph.
- Write actions in criteria language, naming the criterion each one serves.
- Rank them. Eight days is not long.
- Accept the triage. Where you land says nothing about you, only about the draft today.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Complete draft, reviewed against every criterion, and an action list that names criteria and ranks the work. |
| **Secure** | Complete draft, most criteria reviewed, action list present but unranked. |
| **Developing** | Partial draft, or a review that reads for style rather than against criteria. |
| **Not yet** | No draft, or nothing written down to act on. |
