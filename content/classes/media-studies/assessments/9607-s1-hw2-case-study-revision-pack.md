# Media Studies · S1 HW2: Case-Study Revision Pack and Essay Plan

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Set | Wed 2026-12-23 |
| Due | Mon 2027-01-04 |
| What | A revision pack, plus one Section B essay plan |
| Marked on | AO1 |
| Feeds | CS13 directly |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Turn a case-study document into something you can revise from
2. Find your own weakest cell in the coverage matrix
3. Plan a Section B essay aimed at that weakness

## Description

Across the winter break: a **revision pack** built from your case-study document, plus **one Section B essay plan** aimed at **your weakest cell in the coverage matrix**.

Aimed at the weakest cell, not the most comfortable one. The matrix exists so you can see where you are thin, and the point of a plan is to strengthen that, not to rehearse what you already do well.

**It feeds CS13 directly.** The plan you write over the holiday is the one you rehearse against the clock in the first week back. That is not a coincidence, it is the design.

## Guidelines

- Start from the coverage matrix and identify the weakest cell honestly.
- Revision pack: condensed, usable, something you could read in twenty minutes.
- Include named texts, named institutions, named details. Vague notes revise nothing.
- One essay plan, on the weak cell, with a line of argument and three supporting points.
- Name the case studies you would use for each point.
- Keep it. You will need it on Wed 01-06.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A genuinely usable revision pack with specific evidence, and a plan targeting a real weakness with a clear line of argument and named case studies. |
| **Secure** | Pack made and plan written, though the cell chosen is a comfortable one or the evidence is general. |
| **Developing** | Notes copied rather than condensed, or a plan with a topic but no argument. |
| **Not yet** | No pack, or no plan, or a plan on a cell you were already strong in. |
