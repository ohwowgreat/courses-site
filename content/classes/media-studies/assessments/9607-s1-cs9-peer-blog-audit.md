# Media Studies · S1 CS9: Peer Blog Audit

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Wed 2026-10-21, in class, in the double |
| What | A partner's blog audited post by post against the mid-point checklist |
| Marked on | AO3 |
| Feeds | A2, due the following Monday |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Audit work against a checklist rather than against taste
2. Write findings as actions, not as comments
3. Leave with a written list you can act on before A2

## Description

You audit a partner's blog **post by post** against the **mid-point checklist**, using **describe, question, suggest**.

Findings are written **as actions, not as comments**. "Post 3 has no audience justification, add two sentences naming the target audience" is an action. "Post 3 could be better" is a comment, and it helps nobody.

The checklist you use here is the same instrument A2 is marked with, and A2 is due the following Monday. You leave this session with your own action list and the weekend to work it.

## Guidelines

- Go post by post in order. Do not skim for an overall impression.
- Use the protocol: describe what is there, question what is unclear, suggest one specific change.
- Every finding names the post and the fix.
- Be useful rather than kind. A generous audit that finds nothing costs your partner marks.
- Start your own action list in class, from the audit you receive.
- Blogs to the mid-point standard by Monday.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Every post covered against the checklist, findings written as specific actions naming post and fix, and your own action list started before you leave. |
| **Secure** | Most posts covered, findings mostly actionable, action list begun. |
| **Developing** | Coverage patchy or findings written as opinions, so the partner cannot act on them. |
| **Not yet** | Audit not completed, or no written record produced. |
