# Media Studies · S1 CS8: The Technical Lexicon Quiz

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Mon 2026-10-12, in class, 20 minutes |
| What | Thirty items across all four technical elements |
| Marked on | AO1 |
| Self-marked | Immediately, against the definitions on screen |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Define thirty technical terms without notes
2. Label an example correctly by its term
3. Find and close your own vocabulary gaps before A1

## Description

Thirty items, **define-the-term** and **label-the-example**, drawn from all four technical elements plus the semiotic base and the Cambridge glossary.

At least four items are deliberately drawn from sound, the element candidates most often neglect.

You self-mark immediately against the definitions on screen, and correct every missed term into your lexicon. The correcting is the part that matters. A quiz you mark and forget teaches nothing.

## Guidelines

- No notes. The point is to find out what is actually in your head.
- Twenty minutes for thirty items. Do not stall on one.
- If a term is gone, leave it and come back.
- Mark honestly. Nobody sees this but you.
- Every missed term goes into your lexicon with its definition, today.
- Bring your lexicon to the session.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | 26 or more correct, spread across all four elements, and every miss corrected into the lexicon. |
| **Secure** | 20 to 25 correct, with a visible weakness in one element, corrections made. |
| **Developing** | 14 to 19 correct, or gaps concentrated in one element, corrections partial. |
| **Not yet** | Below 14, or the corrections were not made. |

A1 sits two days later. What this finds is what you revise.
