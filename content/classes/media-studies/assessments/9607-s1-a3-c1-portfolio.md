## Objectives

This assessment asks you to show that you can:

1. Finish a media product that works for a real audience, in a real genre, to a standard an audience would accept.
2. Use technical skills well enough that the craft does not distract from the meaning.
3. Show research and planning that visibly shaped what you made.
4. Produce a Creative Critical Reflection that is itself a media product, not an essay in disguise.
5. Analyze your own work honestly, with evidence, including where it did not succeed.
6. Submit complete and on time, with every post dated and every contribution labeled.

## The task

A3 is the whole of Component 1. You submit three things together:

**1. The product.** Your finished film opening or magazine, exported and playable, in the genre you registered.

**2. The Creative Critical Reflection (CCR).** Your reflection **in a creative format**, posted. It answers the four set questions. It is assessed on its own technical and creative skill as well as on what it says. A CCR read off a page as a talking head answers the questions while failing the format.

**3. The blog, complete.** Every post, dated on the day, contributions labeled, no gaps. This is the same blog A2 sampled at its mid-point, now finished.

## Rubric

Cambridge's **Component 1 assessment criteria**, 2027 syllabus. Five strands of 10 marks, **50 marks total**: four strands are AO3 Practical Application (40 marks), one is AO2 Evaluation (10 marks).

| Strand | AO | What it judges | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AO3 | **Use appropriate media language** to communicate meaning to an intended audience | 10 |
| 2 | AO3 | **Use technical skills effectively** to express and communicate ideas | 10 |
| 3 | AO3 | **Research, plan and apply** knowledge and understanding | 10 |
| 4 | AO3 | **Creative Critical Reflection skills**: technical and creative skill in the CCR itself | 10 |
| 5 | AO2 | **Analyse and evaluate own work**, supported with relevant textual evidence | 10 |

Every strand runs on the same band ladder: **9–10 sophisticated · 7–8 effective or thorough · 5–6 clear · 3–4 limited or general · 1–2 basic or minimal · 0 no creditable content.** Strands are banded separately, so a strong product does not carry a weak CCR.

### The bands, strand by strand

| Marks | Media language (1) | Technical skills (2) | Research and planning (3) | CCR skills (4) | Evaluation (5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **9–10** | Sophisticated use of media language, insightful engagement with audience; targets the intended audience with sophistication and is **entirely convincing** as a film opening or magazine | Production is sophisticated, highly appropriate, and accurately follows or **knowingly subverts** genre codes; technical elements sophisticatedly handled, finished to a high standard | Sophisticated research into professional products; varied and detailed planning **fully** meeting the brief; sophisticated implementation showing insightful understanding of the key concepts | Sophisticated technical skills throughout, with a **wide range** of creative approaches, sophisticatedly engaging the audience | Sophisticated understanding of how meaning is created; insightful critical comment on own successes, fully supported with a wide range of examples; sophisticated engagement with **all four** questions |
| **7–8** | Effective use of media language, appropriate engagement; targets audience effectively and is effective in convincing | Production is effective and effectively follows or subverts genre codes; technical elements effectively handled, **mostly** finished to a high standard | Thorough research; effective planning appropriate to the brief; thorough implementation showing effective understanding | Effective technical skills throughout, with **a range** of creative approaches, effectively engaging the audience | Thorough understanding; appropriate critical comment, effectively supported with a range of examples; thorough engagement with all four questions |
| **5–6** | Clear use of media language, **some** engagement; targets audience appropriately and is **mostly** convincing | Production is appropriate and mostly follows or subverts genre codes; technical elements effectively handled, **sometimes** finished to a high standard | Clear research; appropriate planning meeting the brief; implementation showing clear understanding | Clear technical skills, with **more than one** creative approach, mostly engaging the audience | Clear understanding; appropriate critical comment, appropriately supported with examples; clear engagement with all four questions |
| **3–4** | Limited use of media language, **general** engagement; simply targets audience, generally appropriate as a film opening or magazine | Production is limited, generally follows or subverts codes **with limited success**; technical elements generally handled, production is complete | Limited research; generally appropriate planning meeting **some** requirements; implementation showing limited understanding | Limited technical skills, **often only one** creative approach, sometimes engaging the audience | Limited understanding; generally supported with limited examples; limited engagement, with some **uneven coverage** |
| **1–2** | Basic use of media language, **minimal** engagement; only basic understanding of intended audience, may lack relevance as a film opening or magazine | Production is basic, minimal understanding of codes; technical elements limited in success, production **may not be complete** | Basic research; limited planning meeting **few** requirements; implementation showing minimal understanding | Basic technical skills, **only one** creative approach, limited appeal to the audience | Minimal understanding; basic or obvious comment, minimal selection of examples; basic engagement, uneven coverage likely |
| **0** | No creditable content | No creditable content | No creditable content | No creditable content | No creditable content |

Three things are worth reading twice:

- **"Knowingly subverts" sits at the top with "accurately follows."** Breaking a convention on purpose and being able to say why scores as well as observing it perfectly.
- **The CCR is marked twice**: once for its own technical and creative skill (strand 4, AO3) and once for the quality of the evaluation in it (strand 5, AO2). A creative CCR that says nothing loses strand 5. A thoughtful CCR delivered as a read-aloud essay loses strand 4.
- **"All four questions"** is the phrase in strand 5's top three bands. Uneven coverage caps that strand at 3 or 4, however good the strongest answer is.

Marks are curved under [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the BNDS framework]] rather than reported raw.

## After the sit

- **Returned Tue 2026-12-01.** The protocol is fixed: **you band your own portfolio against these criteria before any mark is discussed.** The conversation is about the gap between your band and the mark, not about the mark.
- You then write **one production target**: specific, evidenced from the feedback, achievable in Semester 2. "Hold focus in low light" beats "get better at filming". It is logged as LB4 and collected on in S2.
- The next assessment, [[classes/media-studies/assessments/9607-s1-a4-section-a-authentic-format|A4]], sits Wed 2026-12-16.
