# Media Studies · S1 Assessments

Full register for G11 (AS) Semester 1, 2026-27: **4 attainments + 15 Course Skills items (CS1–CS13 + HW1–HW2) + 5 Learning Behaviour postings + 1 End of Term Exam** across 21 weeks. Weights follow the [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|BNDS framework]]: Attainment 50% · Course Skills 20% · Learning Behaviour 10% · End of Term 20%.
