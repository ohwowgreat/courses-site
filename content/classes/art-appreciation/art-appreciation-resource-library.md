# Art Appreciation · Resource Library

## Readings — assigned

| Reading | Where it lands |
|---|---|
| **John Berger, "The Eaters and the Eaten"** | The anchor of the whole food strand. Its opening line, *"To eat is to appropriate, to take into ourselves the lives of others,"* heads the Food and Ethics unit and appears in four of its lessons. Serves U3. |
| **John Berger, *Ways of Seeing*, ch. 1** | [[classes/art-appreciation/lesson-plans/art-appreciation-s1-lesson-10-berger-and-the-reproduction\|L10 Berger and the reproduction]]. The lesson is named for it. |
| **Walter Benjamin, "The Work of Art in the Age of Its Technological Reproducibility" (2nd version, 1936/2008)** | [[classes/art-appreciation/lesson-plans/art-appreciation-s1-lesson-11-aura-and-the-poor-image\|L11 Aura and the poor image]], the aura half. |
| **Hito Steyerl, "In Defense of the Poor Image"** | L11, the poor-image half. Read the two texts against each other. |
| **Susan Sontag, "In Plato's Cave"** | [[classes/art-appreciation/lesson-plans/art-appreciation-s1-lesson-12-photography-and-after\|L12 Photography and after]]. This is the first essay of *On Photography*, extracted. The full book is on the reference shelf. |
| **Dean Kissick, "The Painted Protest"** · **"Hello Darkness"** | Contemporary art-world criticism. Fits [[classes/art-appreciation/lesson-plans/art-appreciation-s1-lesson-13-the-artworld-and-the-brief\|L13 The artworld and the brief]]. |

## Further reading

| Reading | Note |
|---|---|
| **Michael Baxandall, *Painting and Experience in Fifteenth-Century Italy*** | The source of "painting is a deposit of a social relationship." Underpins the whole patronage strand. |
| **Francis Haskell, *Patrons and Painters: Art and Society in Baroque Italy*** | Baroque patronage. The 25-page extract, pp. 120–145, is the teachable unit; the full book is the shelf copy. |
| **Sarah Thornton, *Seven Days in the Art World*** | Ethnography of the contemporary art world, the empirical counterpart to Danto's philosophy at L13. |
| **Susan Sontag, *On Photography*** (full) | The complete book. "In Plato's Cave" is its first essay. |
| **John Berger, *Portraits*** | |
| **The Image Object Post-Internet** | Post-internet art theory. Pairs with the Ulman and Horvitz videos. |
| **Documents of Contemporary Art** — 16 volumes: Abstraction · Appropriation · Beauty · Documentary · Failure · Nature · Networks · Sound · Systems · The Archive · The Artist's Joke · The Cute · The Everyday · The Sublime · Time · Utopias | The Whitechapel/MIT anthology series. A **thematic shelf, not a syllabus**: each volume is an edited collection of short primary texts. Use it to find one or two excerpts when your Atlas Book theme needs theory. |

The DOCA series is the most reusable thing here for the **Atlas Book capstone**. If you are working on masks and identity, or screens, or ruins, start with a volume rather than an open search.
