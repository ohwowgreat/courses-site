# Art Appreciation · S1 CS2: Ten-Frame Storyboard

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Date | Tue 2026-09-29, made in class |
| What | Ten frames, no captions, no dialogue |
| Test | A partner reads it cold |
| Rehearses | A2 |

## Objectives

By the end of this task you can:

1. Carry a story across ten still frames using framing alone
2. Put time into a picture without writing a word
3. Find the frame where your own sequence stops being readable

## Description

Ten frames a stranger can read in the right order, with **no captions and no dialogue**. Everything the reader needs has to be in the framing: what is close, what is far, what is left out, what repeats.

The swap is the test. A partner reads yours cold, in silence, and tells you the story back. Where they stall is where the sequence fails. That is information, not failure.

This feeds A2, because both are about reading what a picture decided. The sequence you make here is the one the break homework extends.

## Guidelines

- Ten frames. Not nine, not twelve.
- No words anywhere: no captions, no speech, no labels, no titles.
- Stick figures are fine. This is not a drawing test.
- Change the framing between frames. Ten identical wide shots is one frame drawn ten times.
- Decide where your story turns, and give that turn its own frame.
- Before you swap, look at your own sequence once and mark the frame you are least sure about.

## Rubric

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A partner reads the order and the turn correctly with no help. Framing changes carry the meaning, and at least one frame is doing work no other frame does. |
| **Secure** | The partner gets the story with one question. The sequence is legible but one or two frames repeat what a neighbour already said. |
| **Developing** | The partner gets the general subject but not the order or the turn. Framing stays mostly the same throughout. |
| **Not yet** | Fewer than ten frames, or words used to carry the story, or the partner cannot reconstruct it. |

You are not marked on drawing quality. You are marked on whether a stranger can read it.
