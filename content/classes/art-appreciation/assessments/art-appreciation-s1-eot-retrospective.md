## Objectives

This assessment asks you to show that you can:

1. Lay the semester out and see it **as one body of work rather than four units**.
2. **Defend your three works to someone who chose differently**, and not simply concede.
3. Show a semester of looking as one argument, with the argument in the layout.

## The task

The whole semester, read back as one argument.

This is the **third constellation of the year**, and it is the one where the method should finally look like a method rather than a rule you were following.

Over the break you chose **three works that changed how you see**, with reasons. That choice is the seed. In the session you build a constellation that makes an argument about the semester itself: what you now see that you did not see in September, and what the sequence of the year did to your looking.

Everything you have made is available. The Unit 1 boards and the Unit 4 syntheses are back on the walls before Monday, so your earlier work is in the room with you.

## Instructions

1. **Bring your three works and your reasons.** This is the break homework and it is your entry ticket, worth 3 to 4 hours across the holiday.
2. **Build the constellation.** Adjacency is still the argument, as it has been since A1.
3. **Use your own earlier work.** The boards and syntheses on the walls are material, not decoration.
4. **Be ready to defend.** Somebody in the room chose differently, and you will be asked to hold your ground rather than politely agree. Conceding right away is not generosity, it is a failure to argue.
5. **Write the argument** the layout makes.

**If you are absent.** Tell your teacher **at least 24 hours before**. Illness needs a doctor's note. Without advance contact the mark may be 0, even for genuine illness (AL Student Guide p.8). This is a 20% category and there is no second sitting.

## Rubric

Five criteria, equally weighted. The first four are the constellation skills the course has built since September. The fifth is new and only possible at the end.

| | **Selection** | **Adjacency reasoning** | **Layout legibility** | **The written argument** | **Defending your choices** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Perceptive** | Three works chosen because of what they changed, not because they were liked. The reasons are specific to the change | Every pairing carries a reason belonging to that pair alone. The semester's tensions are held rather than smoothed | The argument about the semester is visible on arrival. The layout is doing work the writing does not have to repeat | Argues what the semester did to your looking, and names what is still unresolved | Holds a position under real disagreement, gives ground only for a reason, and can say what would change their mind |
| **Convincing** | Three well-chosen works with clear reasons | Reasons specific to their pairs, one or two transferable | The argument reads clearly with a moment's looking | A clear argument about the semester, supported | Defends the choices with reasons, with some retreat under pressure |
| **Competent** | Three works chosen on preference, with reasons attached afterwards | Related pairings with true but general reasons | An order is visible; the argument needs help | States what changed, supports part of it | Explains the choices, concedes quickly |
| **Partial** | Three works, reasons thin | Grouped by theme | Reads as a collection of the year's work | Narrates the semester | Cannot say why these three rather than three others |
| **Undeveloped** | Selection incomplete or arbitrary | No reasoning | No argument discernible | Little writing | No defense offered |

Under the [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|BNDS framework]] this becomes a curved grade rather than a raw score.

## Why it is in class and not in the exam window

The school exam window is Mon 2027-01-11 to Mon 2027-01-18. This retrospective sits **Tue 2027-01-05**, in class, before it.

The assessment is a constellation build. It needs the room, the walls, the pinned earlier work, and the students arguing with each other. None of that survives being moved into an exam hall, and a two-hour silent sitting would not measure it better.

## After the sit

- The protocol, as always: **criteria**, **ten minutes of silence**, **one named strength**, **one move.**
- **LB9 posts Tue 2027-01-19**, after the Wed 2027-01-20 grades deadline, so it carries no Semester 1 weight and counts toward Semester 2.
- Semester 2 opens on the art-world arc and the Atlas Book capstone. The retrospective is where its first material comes from.
