# Unit 4: Images, Mediation and Modernity

[[classes/art-appreciation/art-appreciation|Art Appreciation]] · [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 4 of 5 · [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-s1-unit-3-food-the-table-and-ethics|← Unit 3]] | [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-s1-unit-5-semester-close-and-retrospective|Unit 5 →]]

## What this unit does

This unit teaches the theorist spine of the course, in this order:

- **John Berger**: a reproduction is not a neutral copy. It changes what a painting means.
- **Walter Benjamin** and **aura**: the special presence of a unique work, tied to its one place and time. A reproduction loses that aura. Because ukiyo-e prints come first, you meet this as a plain fact: a print is already many.
- **Hito Steyerl** and the **poor image**: the low-quality, much-copied image, defended and then made in the degradation lab.
- **Susan Sontag**: photographing is an act on the world, not just a record of it.
- **Clement Greenberg** and **avant-garde and kitsch**: the split between advanced art and mass-produced imitation, tested to where the line stops holding.
- **Arthur Danto** and the **artworld**: art is defined by a shared context of theory and history, shown through Warhol and Duchamp.

The A4 synthesis is your second constellation of the year, arguing across the whole unit. The unit's last lesson sets the retrospective brief as break homework, which belongs to Unit 5's End of Term.
