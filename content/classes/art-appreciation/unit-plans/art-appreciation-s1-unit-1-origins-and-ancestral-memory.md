# Unit 1: Origins and Ancestral Memory

[[classes/art-appreciation/art-appreciation|Art Appreciation]] · [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-semester-1-plan|Semester 1 Plan]] · Unit 1 of 5 · next: [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-s1-unit-2-narrative-myth-and-allegory|Unit 2 →]]

## What this unit covers

This unit introduces the course's method: Warburg's atlas — thematic and non-linear, grouping images by resemblance rather than by date. You work from the earliest images (Chauvet, Lascaux) through Neolithic settlement and ritual objects, focusing on what an object does, not just what it depicts. You then build the first board, a small-scale version of the move you repeat later in A4 and the End-of-Term retrospective. The darkroom session on day three shows how an image is something light did to a surface, which prepares you for Unit 4 in December.

- Key theorist: Aby Warburg.
- Key material: cave imagery, Neolithic figures, ritual objects.
