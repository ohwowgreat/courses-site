## Standing protocols

Session spine (45'): 5 land / 10 look / 20 work / 10 close. At the close, one student says what changed and one says what is stuck. This is not optional.

Critique runs in order: **describe → account → judge, never out of order**. ESL adaptation: you write the describe round before you speak it.

Assessment review comes within 5 working days: criteria, then 10 minutes of silence to find your own gap, then one strength and one move, written. The next lesson opens with the move.

Homework is 30 minutes a day at most. Break homework is 3–4 hours per ten-day holiday.
