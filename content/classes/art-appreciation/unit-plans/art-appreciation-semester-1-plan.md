> [[classes/art-appreciation/art-appreciation|Art Appreciation]] · Semester 1 · next: [[classes/art-appreciation/unit-plans/art-appreciation-semester-2-outline|S2 outline]]

This is a mixed G11 and G12 class, running in Autumn 2026-27. It has 15 lessons across 65 teaching days and 82 periods. Each lesson is built around one deliverable, not one day: if a day is lost, the deliverable stays and the work compresses. Dates follow the [[calendar|school calendar]].

Because the class mixes both grades, absence blocks for either grade affect it. G11 have Juniors Days (11-04 to 06, 12-02 to 04). G12 have Seniors' Days (the week of 10-26, and 11-26 to 27).

## Standing protocols

Session spine (45'): 5 minutes land, 10 look, 20 work, 10 close. At the close, one student says what changed and one says what is stuck; this is not optional. Critique runs in one order, **describe, account, judge**, and never out of order; you write the describe round before you speak it. Assessment review comes within 5 working days: you get the criteria and 10 minutes of silence to find your own gap, then you write one strength and one move. The next lesson opens with that move. Homework is up to 30 minutes a day; break homework is 3–4 hours per ten-day holiday.
