## What you will be able to do

By the end of these sessions, you can:

1. Name the one move that would have made your strongest piece sharper, on your own
2. Set a Semester 2 goal that is about a practice rather than about a grade
3. Act on feedback in the room rather than just filing it

This is three days after the exam window and after the grades deadline, so nothing here counts toward your Semester 1 grade.

## How it runs

- **Day 1 · Tue 19 Jan** — work returned; one-to-one feedback while everyone else re-reads their own work against the criteria.
- **Day 2 · Wed 20 Jan** — one-to-one continued. Grades and comments due.
- **Day 3 · Thu 21 Jan** — Semester 2 preview: patronage, the market, and who decides what is worth money; write your goal. LB9 posted.

## Assessment

- **LB9** — posted after the Wednesday grades deadline, so it counts toward Semester 2, not Semester 1.
- Your Semester 2 goal is the deliverable — about a practice rather than about a grade.
