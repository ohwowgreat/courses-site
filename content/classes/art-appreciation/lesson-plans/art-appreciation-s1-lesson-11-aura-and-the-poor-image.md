## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Define aura precisely enough to disagree with Benjamin about it
2. Defend a low-resolution image as having value of its own, not merely as a compromise
3. Degrade an image deliberately and describe exactly what changed, not that it got worse

## How it runs
