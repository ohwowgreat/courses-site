## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

1. Plan a meal as a work, with decisions you can defend rather than preferences
2. Ask what the abundance cost and who paid, of a picture that does not want the question
3. Write about your own table without turning it into a memory piece

## About this week

Each of the five days stands on its own. Nothing carries from one day to the next, and nothing here is assessed cumulatively. The through-line is thematic, not sequential: five different ways into the same question, any of which works alone. If you miss a day, you have missed that day's content, not a running argument.

## How it runs

- **Day 1 · Mon 26 Oct · `U3·L6`** — shared-lunch prep: the meal as artwork, planned in pairs, decisions defended rather than preferred.
- **Day 2 · Tue 27 Oct · `U3·L7`** — still life and the ethics of abundance: what did this cost, who paid.
- **Day 3 · Wed 28 Oct · `U3·L8`** — individual reflection: your own table, without turning it into a memory piece. You finish the deliverable today.
- **Day 4 · Thu 29 Oct · `U3·L9`** — what a shared meal makes visible that talking does not.
- **Day 5 · Fri 30 Oct · `U3·L10`** — seminar: is hospitality generosity or obligation. LB4 posted.
