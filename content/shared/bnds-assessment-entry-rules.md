# BNDS Assessment Entry Rules

These rules set when graded items may sit and how often each class must post a grade to the ManageBac gradebook. They apply to every course. They work alongside [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the assessment framework]], which governs categories and weights, and sit under [[calendar|the academic calendar]], which owns the fixed dates.

## The rules, as given

> 31 August-4 September: no tests or quizzes.
>
> First attainment: should be entered in MB by 18 September where possible.
>
> At least one MB entry per week for each class. This may be an attainment, Core Skill or Learning Behavior.

## What each one means in practice

**1. Quiet opening week.** No test or quiz may sit between **Mon 2026-08-31 and Fri 2026-09-04**. This covers the first three teaching days (Tue 09-01 to Fri 09-04). Teaching, homework, and ungraded classwork are not affected. A timed in-class written task counts as a test here.

**2. First attainment by Fri 2026-09-18, where possible.** The gradebook should hold an attainment within the first three weeks. Some courses cannot meet this. If a course's first attainment needs content that has not yet been taught, it enters that attainment later, because testing untaught material would be worse than a late date.

**3. One ManageBac entry every teaching week.** Every class needs at least one graded item reaching ManageBac each teaching week. Any category counts: Attainment, Course Skills, or Learning Behaviour. Weeks that fall entirely inside a holiday need nothing. A Learning Behaviour entry is often the way a week with no other assessment gets covered.
