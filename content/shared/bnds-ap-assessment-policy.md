# BNDS AP Assessment and Grading Policy

This is the grading scheme for the **AP Programme**. It is a separate document from the [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|A-Level assessment framework]], with different categories, different weights, a different grade scale, and a different pass boundary. One is not a version of the other.

The policy's stated aim is to promote mastery of skills, fairness, and alignment with AP exam expectations, and to keep grading consistent across all AP courses.

> [!important] Which policy applies to you depends on your programme, not your classroom.
> Art Appreciation teaches both cohorts in one room, so both schemes run there at once. If you are an AP student, you are graded under this page. If you are an A-Level student, you are graded under [[shared/bnds-assessment-framework|the A-Level framework]].

## GPA breakdown

### Semester 1

| Category | Sub-category | What counts | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Majors (50%)** | Assessments | **3–4 items, all equally weighted.** Chapter tests / quizzes / essays / other | 30% |
| | Performance Tasks | **Minimum of 2, all equally weighted.** Projects / labs / essays / other | 20% |
| **Minors (30%)** | — | Homework, quizzes, and classwork supporting course objectives | 30% |
| **Semester Final Exam (20%)** | — | Modeled on AP exam structure, to prepare you for external exams | 20% |

The item counts are part of the policy: Assessments needs **3 or 4** items, and Performance Tasks needs **at least 2**.

There is **no Learning Behaviour category** here, but participation is still graded. It falls inside **Minors**, whose definition, "homework, quizzes, and classwork supporting course objectives", covers seminar postings alongside smaller written tasks. Nothing stops being assessed.

### Semester 2

Semester 2 weights are not yet set in the policy. Anything scheduled for spring under this framework is provisional and will be confirmed once the Semester 2 section is issued.

## Grading scale

The policy's stated reason is to reduce unnecessary stress and maintain academic rigor.

| Letter grade | Percentage range |
|---|---|
| A+ | 95 – 100 |
| A | 90 – 94 |
| A− | 85 – 89 |
| B | 80 – 84 |
| C | 70 – 79 |
| D | 60 – 69 |
| F | 59 or below |

Grades round at **.50 and above**.

There is **no curve**. The bands are applied as they stand.

## Late assignments

**Excused absence.** If your work is late because of an excused absence, you get, after you return, a number of days equal to the days you missed (**maximum 5**) to turn in everything that fell due while you were away. This extension **MAY** go beyond the semester's final assignment deadline.

**Any other reason.** A **10% deduction for every day late, to a maximum of 50% on day 5.** This extension may **NEVER** go beyond the semester's final assignment deadline.

## Scheduling constraint

> No major assignments may be assigned over an SAT/ACT weekend and no major assessments may be given on the Monday or Tuesday following an SAT/ACT weekend.

SAT/ACT dates are listed on the [[calendar|school academic calendar]].
