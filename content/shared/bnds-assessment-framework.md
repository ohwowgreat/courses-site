# BNDS Assessment Framework

The grading scheme for the **A-Level Programme**, from the **BNDS A-Level Student Guide 2025-26**.

> [!important] This scheme is not school-wide.
> The AP Programme runs a different scheme, with different categories, weights, grade bands and late rules. It is recorded at [[shared/bnds-ap-assessment-policy|BNDS AP Assessment and Grading Policy]].

**Edition:** 2025-26, the current one. Its content stays valid except where it touches syllabus specifics, which change per subject and per exam year. Treat the framework, curving, notice, retake and AI rules as live. Check anything syllabus-shaped against the syllabus.

## Grade composition

| Category | Weight | Definition (per handbook) |
|---|---|---|
| **Attainment** | **50%** | Major, summative. Syllabus content only, exam conditions, curved to CIE past-paper data; should produce a reliable Forecast Grade. Project completions and portfolio milestones count. |
| **Course Skills** | **20%** | Minor, formative. Skills and content relevant to the syllabus, flexible format: quizzes, drafts, practice tasks, homework. |
| **Learning Behaviour** | **10%** | Glossary upkeep, blog punctuality, screening and feedback participation, case-study contributions, correcting work, submitting on time. |
| **End of Term Exam** | **20%** | Summative, previously covered content, sat in the school exam window (S1 2026-27: Mon 2027-01-11 – Mon 2027-01-18). |

## Notice rule

You get at least one week's notice before an attainment (Guide p.13). For non-attainment assessments you get 72 hours' notice.

## Standing rules

- **4–6 attainments per semester**; none in the first two teaching weeks.
- **Attainment dropping.** One attainment can be dropped per semester, late in the semester, but only if your class has **at least 6 attainment grades in MB**, you have missed none, have no plagiarism or cheating record, no unexcused absences, are always on time, and attend all required grade and programme meetings (Guide p.12). All four courses run four attainments, so no student in any of them is eligible for this. That comes from course design, not from your conduct.
- **One grade per week** posted on MB: "at least one grade posted per subject each week" (Guide p.12). Learning Behaviour postings fill the weeks with no Course Skills task or attainment.
- **Marking returned within 5 working days**: "Grades will be posted on MB within five working days" (Guide p.12).
- **Homework capped at 30 minutes per subject per day** (Guide p.13). Break homework is about 3–4 hours per ten-day holiday.
- **Extensions** must be requested **at least 24 hours before the deadline** (Guide p.13).
- **Absence from an assessment** requires contacting the subject teacher **24 hours before the due time**; illness needs a doctor's note. *"If no communication is made with the subject teacher in advance you may be given 0, even if this is due to illness"* (Guide p.8).
- **Late-work cutoff:** *"three weeks before the last school day of the semester"* (Guide p.13). For S1 this date falls inside the winter break, so the exact cutoff will be confirmed. See [[calendar|school academic calendar]].
- **Assessment content:** only what has been taught and practised. *"You should have seen similar content or formats beforehand,"* though "the context in which the knowledge or skills are applied may be novel" (Guide p.9).
- A formative Course Skills task set next to an attainment is direct practice for it.
- **Seniors get a reduced assessment load** in the week before the 1 Nov and 1 Dec university deadlines (Guide p.13). This applies to G12 Media Studies and to [[classes/oxbridge/oxbridge|Oxbridge]].

## Curving and grade boundaries

Attainments are **not recorded as raw marks** (Guide pp.11–12):

- Curved against **CIE past-paper data**, "allowing for where students are on their learning trajectory."
- **More generous in G10, stricter in G11** and later in the year. This matters for [[classes/pre-a-level-art-design/pre-a-level-art-design|PAL]], the only G10 course here.
- Grades "reflect your learning trajectory, not what you would score on a CIE exam today."
- Past-paper grade thresholds are "the best starting point" for setting boundaries.
- **Moderation is required** where an attainment runs across multiple classes taught by different teachers. The Head of Department checks large between-class differences and may adjust curves or boundaries.
- *"You will always be told how grade boundaries and curves are decided."*

An attainment "should make a reliable **Forecast Grade**" (Guide p.11).

## Grade conversion (Guide p.11)

| CIE scale | A-Level grade | BNDS GPA |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | A* | 4.3 |
| 80–89 | A | 4.0 |
| 70–79 | B | 3.5 |
| 60–69 | C | 3.0 |
| 50–59 | D | 2.5 |
| 40–49 | E | 2.0 |
| below 40 | U | 0 |

**Failing is below 50%** (Guide p.15). **At or below 70%** triggers the Academic Concern Steps: your advisor and the A-Level Director are informed and support measures follow (Guide p.13).

## Retake policy (Guide §IV, pp.15–16)

You get one opportunity to retake a failed course, in the holiday after the semester, with 2–3 teacher meetings for support. The work is "just as challenging" and similar in quantity to the original. The grading policy is explained before finals and **cannot be changed once agreed**. A signed academic-honesty declaration is required; plagiarism means immediate failure with no resubmission.

**A passed retake earns the equivalent of a D**, not the grade actually achieved. A failed retake leaves the original grade standing. Under force majeure, more than 50% completion plus written parent-signed evidence can pass; without evidence, fail.

Graduating seniors get only **two weeks** for a retake, because their Summer Holiday starts early.

## Category definitions

The handbook's own wording (Guide p.11):

- **Attainment 50%:** "Tests only on syllabus content · Done under exam conditions · Curved according to CIE past paper data, allowing for where students are on their learning trajectory (ie more generous curves for G10) · It should make a reliable Forecast Grade." Assessment types: past paper questions.
- **Course Skills 20%:** "Testing on skills and content relevant to the syllabus · Format more flexible than attainment." Types: quizzes, presentations, homework.
- **Learning Behaviour 10%:** "Tracking and incentivising important learning behaviours for the class eg communication, collaboration, inquisitivity." Types: participation, asking questions in class, group work, independent inquiry, correcting work, submitting work on time.
- **End of Term Exam 20%:** "Summative, on previously covered syllabus content." Types: past paper questions.

The handbook adds that "Departments may decide more precisely what are deemed acceptable assessments for these different categories." This is the clause under which A Level Art & Design's Final and PAL's portfolio sitting count.
